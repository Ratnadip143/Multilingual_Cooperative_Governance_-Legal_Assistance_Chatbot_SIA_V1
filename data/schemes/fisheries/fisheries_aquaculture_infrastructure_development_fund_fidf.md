# Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund (FIDF)

## Scheme ID
17

## Official Name
Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund (FIDF)

## Type
Central Sector Scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying

## Purpose
The Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund (FIDF) was created to provide concessional finance for eligible fisheries infrastructure projects.

The fund aims to strengthen fisheries infrastructure, increase fish production and productivity, improve post-harvest management and support employment and income generation in the fisheries sector.

## Key Objectives
- Create and strengthen fisheries infrastructure
- Increase fish production and productivity
- Improve post-harvest management
- Reduce post-harvest losses
- Promote aquaculture development
- Improve fish handling and processing facilities
- Strengthen fisheries value chains
- Encourage investment in fisheries infrastructure
- Generate employment and livelihood opportunities

## Major Areas of Support
Depending on the applicable guidelines, FIDF may support eligible projects related to:

- Fishing harbours
- Fish landing centres
- Fish markets
- Cold storage and cold-chain facilities
- Fish processing units
- Fish transportation
- Aquaculture infrastructure
- Fish seed farms
- Modernisation of fisheries infrastructure
- Other eligible fisheries infrastructure projects

## Eligible Entities
Depending on the applicable guidelines and project category, eligible entities may include:

- State Governments
- Union Territories
- State-owned corporations
- State Government entities
- Fisheries cooperatives
- Fisheries-related organisations
- Other eligible agencies and institutions

Eligibility is subject to the applicable FIDF guidelines and project requirements.

## Benefits
FIDF provides access to concessional finance for eligible fisheries infrastructure projects.

Support may help eligible entities to:

- Develop fisheries infrastructure
- Modernise existing facilities
- Improve fish handling and storage
- Strengthen cold-chain infrastructure
- Improve fish processing
- Develop aquaculture infrastructure
- Improve market and value-chain facilities
- Create employment opportunities

The exact financial assistance and terms depend on the applicable guidelines and project.

## Financing
FIDF provides concessional finance through the designated financing mechanism for eligible fisheries infrastructure projects.

Project financing is subject to:

- Eligibility conditions
- Project appraisal
- Applicable financing limits
- Interest and repayment conditions
- Lending institution requirements
- Current government guidelines

## Fisheries Cooperatives
Eligible fisheries cooperative societies may access support for approved fisheries infrastructure projects under applicable FIDF provisions.

The project must satisfy the relevant eligibility and financing requirements.

## Infrastructure Development
FIDF focuses on infrastructure that can improve the fisheries value chain.

Eligible infrastructure may include:

- Fish landing facilities
- Fishing harbours
- Fish markets
- Cold-chain facilities
- Processing infrastructure
- Storage facilities
- Transportation facilities
- Aquaculture infrastructure
- Fish seed production facilities

## Application / Access
Eligible entities should approach the designated financing institution and the concerned fisheries authority for accessing FIDF support.

Projects are subject to appraisal and approval according to the applicable FIDF guidelines.

The exact application route may depend on the type of beneficiary and project.

## Documents / Information
Applicants or project entities may be required to provide:

- Entity registration details
- Project proposal
- Detailed project report
- Project cost estimates
- Financial information
- Land or infrastructure details, where applicable
- Bank or financing details
- Other documents required for project appraisal

Exact requirements depend on the project and financing institution.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is FIDF?
FIDF stands for Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund. It provides concessional finance for eligible fisheries and aquaculture infrastructure projects.

### What is the main purpose of FIDF?
The main purpose is to develop and modernise fisheries infrastructure and support production, productivity, post-harvest management and value-chain development.

### Who can benefit from FIDF?
Eligible government entities, fisheries organisations, cooperatives and other approved entities can access financing for eligible infrastructure projects.

### Does FIDF support fisheries infrastructure?
Yes. FIDF supports eligible fisheries infrastructure projects such as harbours, landing centres, markets, cold-chain and processing facilities.

### Does FIDF support aquaculture?
Yes. Eligible aquaculture infrastructure and related projects can receive support under applicable FIDF provisions.

### Does FIDF support fisheries cooperatives?
Eligible fisheries cooperatives can access support for approved infrastructure projects subject to applicable eligibility and financing conditions.

### Does FIDF provide a subsidy?
FIDF primarily provides concessional finance for eligible infrastructure projects. It should not be treated as a general subsidy scheme.

### What type of projects can receive FIDF support?
Eligible projects may include fishing harbours, fish landing centres, fish markets, cold storage, processing facilities, transportation infrastructure, aquaculture infrastructure and fish seed facilities.

### Where can an applicant access FIDF?
Eligible project entities should approach the designated financing institution and concerned fisheries authority for the applicable FIDF process.

### Is every fisheries project eligible for FIDF?
No. Projects must meet the applicable eligibility conditions, infrastructure requirements and financing criteria.
