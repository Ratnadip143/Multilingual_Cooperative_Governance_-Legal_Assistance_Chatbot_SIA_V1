# Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM)

## Scheme ID
19

## Official Name
Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM)

## Type
Central Sector Scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying

## Purpose
The Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM) aims to develop and conserve indigenous bovine breeds and improve the productivity of the country's cattle population.

The mission promotes scientific breeding, genetic improvement, conservation of indigenous breeds and better management of bovine resources to increase milk production and improve the economic condition of farmers.

## Key Objectives
- Develop and conserve indigenous bovine breeds
- Improve genetic quality of cattle
- Increase milk production and productivity
- Promote scientific breeding practices
- Strengthen artificial insemination services
- Improve access to quality breeding resources
- Promote conservation of indigenous cattle
- Support sustainable dairy farming
- Improve farmers' income from dairying

## Major Areas of Support
Depending on the applicable component and guidelines, RGM may support:

- Indigenous breed development
- Breed conservation
- Genetic improvement
- Artificial insemination
- Quality semen production
- Embryo transfer technology
- Sex-sorted semen technology
- Establishment and strengthening of breeding farms
- Gokul Grams
- Training and capacity building
- Dairy farmer support and awareness

## Indigenous Breed Conservation
RGM focuses on conservation and development of indigenous cattle breeds.

The mission promotes:

- Identification and conservation of indigenous breeds
- Scientific breeding
- Genetic improvement
- Better reproductive management
- Preservation of valuable bovine genetic resources

## Artificial Insemination
RGM supports improvement of artificial insemination coverage and access to quality breeding services.

Artificial insemination can help farmers access improved genetic material and contribute to genetic improvement of cattle.

## Gokul Grams
Gokul Grams are intended to support conservation and development of indigenous cattle breeds.

They can provide facilities for:

- Breed conservation
- Scientific cattle management
- Breeding activities
- Training
- Demonstration of improved cattle management practices

## Benefits
Depending on the applicable component, RGM may contribute to:

- Improved cattle productivity
- Better genetic quality
- Conservation of indigenous breeds
- Access to improved breeding services
- Increased milk production
- Better dairy farming practices
- Improved farmer income
- Development of breeding infrastructure
- Capacity building and training

The exact assistance depends on the component and applicable government guidelines.

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Depending on the applicable component, support may involve:

- Dairy farmers
- Livestock farmers
- Cattle breeders
- Farmer groups
- Cooperatives
- Milk producer organisations
- State livestock departments
- Breeding organisations
- Other eligible institutions and agencies

Eligibility depends on the specific component and implementation guidelines.

## Support for Farmers
Farmers may benefit from RGM through access to improved breeding services, artificial insemination, quality genetic resources and awareness or training programmes.

The availability of particular services depends on the State and implementing agency.

## Application / Access
RGM is implemented through the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, State Animal Husbandry Departments and other designated implementing agencies.

Farmers should approach the concerned State Animal Husbandry Department, veterinary institution or designated implementing agency to access applicable services.

The application or service-access procedure depends on the activity and State.

## Documents / Information
For applicable services or programmes, farmers may be required to provide:

- Farmer details
- Identity information
- Address details
- Cattle or livestock details
- Bank account details, where applicable
- Land or farm details, where applicable
- Other information required by the implementing agency

Exact requirements vary according to the service or component.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is Rashtriya Gokul Mission?
Rashtriya Gokul Mission is a government initiative for conservation and development of indigenous bovine breeds and improvement of cattle productivity.

### What is the main purpose of RGM?
The main purpose is to conserve indigenous cattle breeds, improve their genetic quality and increase milk production and productivity.

### Who implements RGM?
RGM is implemented through the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, State Animal Husbandry Departments and designated agencies.

### Who can benefit from RGM?
Dairy farmers, livestock farmers, cattle breeders and other eligible stakeholders can benefit from applicable RGM activities and services.

### Does RGM support indigenous cattle breeds?
Yes. Conservation and development of indigenous bovine breeds is a major objective of RGM.

### Does RGM support artificial insemination?
Yes. RGM supports improvement and expansion of artificial insemination and other scientific breeding services.

### Does RGM help increase milk production?
Yes. Genetic improvement and better breeding practices under RGM are intended to improve cattle productivity and milk production.

### What are Gokul Grams?
Gokul Grams are facilities intended to support conservation and development of indigenous cattle breeds along with scientific cattle management and breeding activities.

### Does RGM support dairy farmers?
Yes. Dairy farmers can benefit through applicable breeding services, genetic improvement, training and related programmes.

### Where can farmers access RGM services?
Farmers can approach the concerned State Animal Husbandry Department, veterinary institution or designated implementing agency.

### Does RGM provide the same benefit to every farmer?
No. Services and assistance depend on the applicable component, eligibility, State and current government guidelines.
