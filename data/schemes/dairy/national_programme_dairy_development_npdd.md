# National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD)

## Scheme ID
21

## Official Name
National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD)

## Type
Central Sector Scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying

## Purpose
The National Programme for Dairy Development (NPDD) aims to strengthen dairy infrastructure and improve the dairy sector in India.

It focuses on improving milk procurement, processing, chilling, testing, storage and marketing infrastructure while strengthening dairy cooperatives and milk producer institutions.

## Key Objectives
- Increase organized milk procurement
- Strengthen dairy infrastructure
- Improve milk processing capacity
- Improve milk quality and testing
- Strengthen milk chilling facilities
- Support dairy cooperatives and milk producer institutions
- Improve market access for milk producers
- Improve the quality of milk and milk products
- Increase participation of dairy farmers in organized dairy systems
- Improve income opportunities for dairy farmers

## Dairy Infrastructure
NPDD supports development and strengthening of dairy infrastructure.

Eligible activities may include:

- Milk collection centres
- Bulk milk coolers
- Milk chilling facilities
- Dairy processing plants
- Milk testing facilities
- Milk storage facilities
- Milk transportation infrastructure
- Other approved dairy infrastructure

## Milk Quality
The programme supports improvement of milk quality through better collection, chilling, testing and processing systems.

This helps reduce quality deterioration and supports safer and better-quality milk and milk products.

## Dairy Cooperatives
NPDD supports strengthening of organized dairy systems and eligible dairy cooperative institutions.

Support may help improve:

- Milk procurement
- Collection systems
- Chilling
- Processing
- Testing
- Marketing
- Institutional capacity

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Depending on the applicable component, eligible implementing organisations may include:

- State dairy federations
- Dairy cooperative societies
- Milk unions
- Milk producer companies
- Other eligible milk producer institutions
- State implementing agencies
- Other approved organisations

Individual farmer eligibility may depend on the specific component and implementation arrangement.

## Support for Dairy Farmers
Dairy farmers may benefit indirectly or directly through improved milk collection, chilling, testing, processing and marketing systems operated by eligible dairy institutions.

Improved organized dairy infrastructure can provide better access to formal milk markets.

## Application / Access
NPDD is implemented through the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying and eligible State-level dairy institutions and implementing agencies.

Dairy cooperatives, milk unions, State dairy federations and other eligible institutions should follow the applicable government process for seeking support.

Individual farmers generally access programme benefits through participating dairy institutions and milk producer organisations.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is the National Programme for Dairy Development?
NPDD is a government programme aimed at strengthening dairy infrastructure, milk procurement, processing, testing and organized dairy systems.

### What is the main purpose of NPDD?
Its main purpose is to improve dairy infrastructure and strengthen organized milk procurement, processing, quality and marketing systems.

### Who implements NPDD?
NPDD is implemented through the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying and eligible State-level dairy institutions and implementing agencies.

### Does NPDD support dairy cooperatives?
Yes. Strengthening eligible dairy cooperative institutions is an important area of the programme.

### Does NPDD support milk collection?
Yes. Eligible milk collection infrastructure can be supported under applicable components.

### Does NPDD support milk chilling?
Yes. Milk chilling infrastructure such as bulk milk coolers and other approved facilities can be supported.

### Does NPDD support milk processing?
Yes. Eligible dairy processing infrastructure may receive support under applicable components.

### Does NPDD improve milk quality?
Yes. Better collection, chilling, testing and processing infrastructure helps improve milk quality.

### Can individual dairy farmers apply directly?
Programme access for individual farmers depends on the applicable component. Farmers may benefit through participating dairy cooperatives, milk unions and other eligible milk producer institutions.

### What organisations can receive NPDD support?
Eligible State dairy federations, milk unions, dairy cooperatives, milk producer companies and other approved institutions may receive support under applicable components.

### Where can institutions access NPDD support?
Eligible institutions should follow the applicable process through the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying and concerned State implementing authorities.

### Is the same assistance available for every project?
No. Support depends on the project, component, implementing organisation and current government guidelines.
