# Dairy Processing & Infrastructure Development Fund (DIDF)

## Scheme ID
22

## Official Name
Dairy Processing and Infrastructure Development Fund (DIDF)

## Type
Central Sector Scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying

## Purpose
The Dairy Processing and Infrastructure Development Fund (DIDF) was created to strengthen dairy processing infrastructure and improve milk processing facilities in India.

The fund supports modernization and expansion of dairy processing infrastructure, improvement of milk quality and development of related infrastructure to benefit dairy cooperatives and milk producers.

## Key Objectives
- Modernize dairy processing infrastructure
- Increase milk processing capacity
- Improve milk quality
- Strengthen dairy cooperatives
- Improve milk procurement infrastructure
- Support value addition in dairy products
- Improve cold-chain infrastructure
- Strengthen organized dairy systems
- Improve market access for milk producers

## Major Areas
Depending on the applicable project and government guidelines, DIDF supports infrastructure such as:

- Milk processing plants
- Milk chilling facilities
- Milk procurement infrastructure
- Milk testing facilities
- Dairy product manufacturing
- Value addition facilities
- Cold-chain infrastructure
- Milk packaging facilities
- Quality improvement systems
- Other approved dairy infrastructure

## Dairy Processing Infrastructure
DIDF supports modernization and expansion of dairy processing facilities.

Eligible projects may include:

- Expansion of existing dairy plants
- Modernization of processing facilities
- Installation of dairy processing equipment
- Improvement of milk handling systems
- Development of value-added dairy product facilities

## Milk Chilling and Collection
The fund may support infrastructure required for efficient milk collection and chilling.

This can include:

- Bulk milk coolers
- Milk collection centres
- Milk chilling facilities
- Milk transportation infrastructure
- Milk testing equipment

## Dairy Cooperatives
DIDF primarily supports eligible dairy cooperatives, milk unions, State dairy federations and other approved dairy institutions for infrastructure development.

The objective is to strengthen organized dairy systems and improve the services available to milk producers.

## Benefits
Depending on the applicable project, DIDF can support:

- Modernization of dairy plants
- Increased milk processing capacity
- Improved milk quality
- Better milk collection and chilling
- Improved dairy product manufacturing
- Value addition
- Better cold-chain infrastructure
- Strengthened dairy cooperatives
- Improved market access
- Better dairy infrastructure for milk producers

The exact financing and assistance depend on the applicable project and current government guidelines.

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Depending on the applicable guidelines, eligible entities may include:

- State dairy federations
- Dairy cooperative unions
- Dairy cooperative societies
- Milk producer companies
- Other eligible dairy institutions
- Approved implementing agencies

Individual dairy farmers generally benefit through participating dairy cooperatives and milk producer institutions rather than applying directly for infrastructure funding.

## Support for Dairy Farmers
Dairy farmers may benefit indirectly through improved milk procurement, chilling, processing, testing and marketing infrastructure developed by eligible dairy institutions.

Improved dairy infrastructure can help strengthen organized milk markets and support better handling of milk.

## Financing
DIDF was designed as a fund-based infrastructure financing programme for eligible dairy projects.

Financial support and loan terms depend on the applicable government framework, project and implementing institution.

SIA should not state a fixed current interest rate, loan amount or assistance percentage unless it is confirmed from the latest official source.

## Application / Access
Eligible dairy institutions should follow the application and project approval process specified by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying and designated financing or implementing institutions.

Individual farmers generally access benefits through participating dairy cooperatives, milk unions or other eligible dairy institutions.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is DIDF?
DIDF stands for Dairy Processing and Infrastructure Development Fund. It was created to strengthen dairy processing and related infrastructure in India.

### What is the main purpose of DIDF?
Its main purpose is to modernize and strengthen dairy processing, milk collection, chilling and related infrastructure.

### Who can benefit from DIDF?
Eligible dairy cooperatives, milk unions, State dairy federations, milk producer companies and other approved dairy institutions can receive support for eligible projects.

### Does DIDF support dairy cooperatives?
Yes. Strengthening dairy cooperatives and organized dairy institutions is an important focus of DIDF.

### Does DIDF support milk processing?
Yes. Modernization and expansion of milk processing infrastructure are major areas of the fund.

### Does DIDF support milk chilling?
Eligible milk chilling infrastructure such as bulk milk coolers can be supported under applicable projects.

### Does DIDF support milk collection?
Yes. Eligible milk procurement and collection infrastructure can be supported.

### Does DIDF support dairy product manufacturing?
Eligible dairy product processing and value-addition infrastructure can receive support under applicable projects.

### Can an individual farmer directly apply for DIDF?
DIDF is primarily designed for eligible dairy institutions and infrastructure projects. Individual farmers generally benefit through participating dairy cooperatives or milk producer institutions.

### Is DIDF the same as NPDD?
No. NPDD is a programme for dairy development, while DIDF is a fund-based initiative focused on dairy processing and infrastructure financing.

### Where can eligible institutions access DIDF support?
Eligible institutions should follow the applicable process through the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying and designated financing or implementing institutions.

### Is the same financial assistance available for every project?
No. Financing depends on the project, eligible institution, applicable conditions and current government guidelines.
