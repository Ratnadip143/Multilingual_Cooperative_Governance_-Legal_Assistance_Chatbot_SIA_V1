# Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

## Scheme ID
11

## Official Name
Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

## Type
Central Sector Scheme / Agriculture Infrastructure Financing

## Ministry / Organisation
Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

## Purpose
The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) provides medium- to long-term debt financing for investment in post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets.

The scheme aims to improve agricultural infrastructure, reduce post-harvest losses and strengthen the agricultural value chain.

## Key Features
- Medium- and long-term debt financing
- Interest subvention support
- Credit guarantee support for eligible loans
- Support for post-harvest management infrastructure
- Support for community farming assets

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Eligible beneficiaries may include:

- Farmers
- Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
- Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)
- Self Help Groups (SHGs)
- Joint Liability Groups (JLGs)
- Cooperatives
- Start-ups
- Agricultural entrepreneurs
- Other eligible entities as specified under the scheme guidelines

## Supported Infrastructure
AIF can support eligible agricultural infrastructure such as:

- Warehouses
- Cold storage
- Pack houses
- Sorting and grading units
- Primary processing facilities
- Supply-chain infrastructure
- Community farming assets
- Other eligible post-harvest infrastructure

## Benefits
- Improved access to institutional financing
- Interest subvention on eligible loans
- Credit guarantee support for eligible beneficiaries
- Development of post-harvest infrastructure
- Reduction in post-harvest losses
- Better storage, processing and marketing opportunities
- Strengthening of agricultural value chains

## Interest Support
Eligible loans under the scheme receive interest subvention support as provided in the applicable AIF guidelines.

The exact interest benefit, loan period and other financial conditions should be verified from the latest official guidelines.

## Application / Access
Eligible beneficiaries can apply for financing through participating lending institutions and the official AIF online platform.

The application and approval process depends on the eligibility of the project and the applicable scheme guidelines.

## Documents / Information
Applicants may be required to provide information such as:

- Applicant details
- Project details
- Land or asset-related information, where applicable
- Cost estimates
- Business/project plan
- Bank and financial information
- Other documents required by the lending institution

Exact document requirements may vary according to the beneficiary and project.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund?
The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund is a financing facility that supports investment in post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets.

### Who can benefit from the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund?
Farmers, FPOs, PACS, cooperatives, SHGs, JLGs, agricultural entrepreneurs and other eligible entities can benefit subject to the scheme guidelines.

### Can PACS apply under AIF?
Yes. Eligible PACS can access financing under AIF subject to applicable conditions and guidelines.

### What infrastructure is supported under AIF?
The scheme supports eligible post-harvest infrastructure such as warehouses, cold storage, pack houses, sorting and grading facilities, processing infrastructure and other eligible assets.

### Does AIF provide interest support?
Yes. Eligible loans receive interest subvention support according to the applicable AIF guidelines.

### Does AIF provide credit guarantee support?
Credit guarantee support is available for eligible loans subject to the applicable conditions.

### Can FPOs benefit from AIF?
Yes. Eligible FPOs can access financing under the scheme subject to applicable requirements.

### How can I apply for AIF?
Eligible applicants can apply through the official AIF platform and participating lending institutions.

### Does AIF provide grants?
AIF primarily provides debt financing with interest subvention and credit guarantee support for eligible projects. It should not be described as a general grant scheme.

## Important Note
Eligibility, financial limits, interest support, credit guarantee provisions, eligible activities and application procedures may change.

SIA should use the latest official AIF guidelines and government information when answering questions about current scheme conditions.
