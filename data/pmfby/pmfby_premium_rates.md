# PMFBY Premium Rates

## Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) provides crop insurance coverage to farmers against specified crop losses and risks.

## Farmer Premium Rates

Under PMFBY, the maximum premium payable by farmers is:

- **Kharif crops:** 2% of the Sum Insured
- **Rabi crops:** 1.5% of the Sum Insured
- **Annual commercial / annual horticultural crops:** 5% of the Sum Insured

The remaining actuarial premium is shared by the Government as subsidy according to the applicable PMFBY guidelines.

## Important Note

The 2% rate for Kharif crops is the maximum farmer premium share under the standard PMFBY structure. It should not be interpreted as the actual insurance premium charged by the insurance company.

The applicable premium, crop, area, sum insured and subsidy arrangements depend on the notified crop and State/UT under the PMFBY guidelines.

SIA should not give a specific premium amount without knowing the crop, notified area and sum insured.

## Example

If the Sum Insured for a Kharif crop is ₹1,00,000, the farmer's maximum standard premium share would be:

2% of ₹1,00,000 = ₹2,000

The actual applicable amount should be verified against the relevant State/UT notification and PMFBY rules.
