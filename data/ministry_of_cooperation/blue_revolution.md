# Blue Revolution

## Scheme ID
18

## Official Name
Blue Revolution – Integrated Development and Management of Fisheries

## Type
Centrally Sponsored Scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying

## Purpose
The Blue Revolution initiative aims to promote the integrated and sustainable development of the fisheries sector in India.

It focuses on increasing fish production and productivity, improving fisheries infrastructure, strengthening post-harvest management, promoting aquaculture and improving the livelihoods of fishers and fish farmers.

## Key Objectives
- Increase fish production and productivity
- Promote sustainable fisheries development
- Encourage aquaculture development
- Improve fisheries infrastructure
- Strengthen post-harvest management
- Reduce post-harvest losses
- Improve fish handling and processing
- Promote employment and livelihood opportunities
- Improve the income of fishers and fish farmers
- Strengthen fisheries value chains

## Major Areas
Blue Revolution supports eligible fisheries activities such as:

- Inland fisheries
- Marine fisheries
- Aquaculture
- Fish seed production
- Fisheries infrastructure
- Fish landing facilities
- Fish processing
- Cold-chain facilities
- Fish transportation
- Post-harvest management
- Fisheries extension and training
- Welfare and livelihood support

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Depending on the applicable component, beneficiaries may include:

- Fishers
- Fish farmers
- Fish workers
- Fisheries entrepreneurs
- Self Help Groups (SHGs)
- Fisheries cooperative societies
- Fishermen cooperative societies
- Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)
- Other eligible fisheries organisations and institutions

Eligibility depends on the applicable component and government guidelines.

## Benefits
Depending on the component and eligibility, support may include:

- Fisheries production and aquaculture development
- Fisheries infrastructure
- Fish seed production facilities
- Post-harvest infrastructure
- Cold-chain facilities
- Fish processing and value addition
- Fish transportation
- Capacity building and training
- Livelihood and employment support
- Improved market access

The exact assistance depends on the applicable component and current guidelines.

## Aquaculture Development
Blue Revolution promotes development of aquaculture and related activities.

Support may include eligible activities related to:

- Fish farming
- Aquaculture infrastructure
- Fish seed production
- Technology adoption
- Productivity improvement
- Other approved aquaculture activities

## Post-Harvest Management
Blue Revolution supports eligible post-harvest infrastructure to improve fish handling and reduce losses.

This may include:

- Ice plants
- Cold storage
- Refrigerated transportation
- Fish landing centres
- Fish markets
- Processing facilities
- Storage and handling infrastructure
- Value-addition facilities

## Fisheries Infrastructure
The initiative supports development and modernisation of fisheries infrastructure.

Eligible infrastructure may include:

- Fishing harbours
- Fish landing centres
- Fish markets
- Cold-chain infrastructure
- Processing facilities
- Fish seed farms
- Aquaculture facilities
- Transportation and storage facilities

## Fisheries Cooperatives
Eligible fisheries cooperative societies may receive support under applicable fisheries development components.

Support may cover approved infrastructure, production, processing, marketing and other eligible activities.

## Application / Access
Implementation is carried out through the Department of Fisheries, concerned State/UT Fisheries Departments and designated implementing agencies.

Eligible applicants should approach the concerned State/UT Fisheries Department or designated authority for the applicable fisheries development component.

The application procedure depends on the beneficiary category and activity.

## Documents / Information
Applicants may be required to provide:

- Applicant or beneficiary details
- Identity and address information
- Bank account details
- Fisheries or aquaculture activity details
- Land or project details, where applicable
- Project proposal
- Cost estimates
- Registration details, where applicable
- Other documents required by the implementing authority

Exact document requirements may vary according to the component and State/UT.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is Blue Revolution?
Blue Revolution is an initiative for the integrated development and management of fisheries in India.

### What is the main purpose of Blue Revolution?
Its main purpose is to increase fisheries production and productivity while promoting sustainable fisheries, infrastructure development, aquaculture and better livelihoods.

### Who can benefit from Blue Revolution?
Eligible fishers, fish farmers, fish workers, cooperatives, SHGs, FPOs, entrepreneurs and other approved beneficiaries can receive support under applicable components.

### Does Blue Revolution support fish farmers?
Yes. Eligible fish farmers and aquaculture activities can receive support under applicable fisheries development components.

### Does Blue Revolution support aquaculture?
Yes. Aquaculture development is an important area of the initiative.

### Does Blue Revolution support fisheries infrastructure?
Yes. Eligible infrastructure such as landing centres, markets, cold-chain and processing facilities may receive support.

### Does Blue Revolution support fish processing?
Eligible fish processing and value-addition activities can receive support under applicable components.

### Does Blue Revolution support fisheries cooperatives?
Eligible fisheries cooperatives may receive support under applicable components subject to eligibility conditions.

### Does Blue Revolution help reduce post-harvest losses?
Yes. Support for post-harvest infrastructure is intended to improve fish handling and reduce losses.

### Where can applicants apply?
Applicants should approach the concerned State/UT Fisheries Department or designated implementing agency for the relevant component.

### Is the same assistance available for every activity?
No. Assistance varies according to the activity, beneficiary category, component and applicable government guidelines.
