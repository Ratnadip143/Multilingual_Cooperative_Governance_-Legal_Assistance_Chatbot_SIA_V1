# Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY)

## Scheme ID
26

## Official Name
Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY)

## Type
Centrally Sponsored Scheme

## Ministry / Department
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India

## Purpose
Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) aims to provide all-weather road connectivity to eligible unconnected rural habitations.

The scheme focuses on improving rural road connectivity and access to essential services, markets, education, healthcare and economic opportunities.

## Key Objectives
- Provide all-weather road connectivity to eligible rural habitations
- Improve connectivity between villages and nearby markets
- Improve access to schools and healthcare facilities
- Support rural economic development
- Improve transportation and mobility in rural areas
- Strengthen rural infrastructure

## Major Features
- Construction of new rural roads
- Upgradation of eligible rural roads
- Improved connectivity to eligible habitations
- Quality monitoring of rural road works
- Use of technology for planning, monitoring and implementation

## Benefits
PMGSY can improve rural connectivity by providing better roads to eligible habitations.

Improved road connectivity can help rural residents:
- Reach markets more easily
- Access healthcare facilities
- Reach educational institutions
- Improve transportation
- Access employment and economic opportunities
- Improve movement of agricultural and other rural products

## Eligible Areas / Habitations
The scheme focuses on eligible rural habitations that meet the applicable connectivity and population criteria under the relevant PMGSY guidelines.

Eligibility of a habitation depends on the applicable programme phase, guidelines and government criteria.

## Rural Road Connectivity
PMGSY roads are intended to provide reliable connectivity between eligible rural habitations and the existing road network.

The programme may also support upgrading existing rural roads where permitted under the applicable guidelines.

## Implementation
PMGSY is implemented through the Ministry of Rural Development and designated State-level rural development agencies.

State governments and designated implementing agencies are responsible for planning, execution and maintenance according to applicable guidelines.

## Application / Access
PMGSY is primarily an infrastructure programme implemented through government agencies rather than an individual beneficiary application scheme.

Rural residents can raise local road connectivity concerns through the concerned:
- Gram Panchayat
- Block-level authority
- District rural development authority
- State rural roads agency
- Other designated government authority

The exact process depends on the State and applicable PMGSY implementation arrangements.

## Documents / Information
For road proposals and implementation, government authorities may require:
- Details of the habitation
- Population information
- Existing road connectivity details
- Road condition information
- Location and mapping information
- Project and technical details
- Other information required under PMGSY guidelines

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is PMGSY?
PMGSY is a Government of India rural road programme aimed at providing all-weather road connectivity to eligible rural habitations.

### What is the main purpose of PMGSY?
The main purpose is to improve rural road connectivity and provide reliable road access to eligible unconnected habitations.

### Who benefits from PMGSY?
Residents of eligible rural habitations benefit from improved road connectivity and access to markets, education, healthcare and other services.

### Does PMGSY provide roads to villages?
Yes. The programme supports rural road connectivity for eligible habitations according to applicable guidelines.

### Does PMGSY help farmers?
Improved rural roads can help farmers transport agricultural products to markets and access services more easily.

### Can an individual apply for PMGSY?
PMGSY is primarily implemented as a government infrastructure programme. Individuals generally do not receive direct construction assistance under the scheme.

### How can a village raise a road connectivity issue?
Residents can approach the Gram Panchayat or concerned block, district or State rural road authority regarding local road connectivity issues.

### Does PMGSY cover road maintenance?
Maintenance responsibilities and provisions depend on the applicable PMGSY guidelines and implementation arrangements.

### Is PMGSY available across India?
PMGSY is a national rural road programme implemented across eligible States and Union Territories according to applicable guidelines.

## Important Note
Eligibility of habitations, road selection, construction standards, funding arrangements and implementation procedures can vary according to the current PMGSY guidelines and programme phase.

SIA should use the latest official PMGSY information when answering questions about specific eligibility, road proposals or implementation details.
