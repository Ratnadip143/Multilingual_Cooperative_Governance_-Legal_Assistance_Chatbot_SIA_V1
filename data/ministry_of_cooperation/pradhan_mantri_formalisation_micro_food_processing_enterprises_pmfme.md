# Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME)

## Scheme ID
15

## Official Name
Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME) Scheme

## Type
Centrally Sponsored Scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)

## Purpose
The Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME) Scheme aims to support the formalisation and upgradation of micro food processing enterprises.

The scheme provides support for improving food processing businesses, strengthening infrastructure, improving access to finance, and developing capabilities of micro food processing enterprises.

## Key Objectives
- Support formalisation of micro food processing enterprises
- Improve existing food processing units
- Promote technology upgradation
- Improve access to institutional credit
- Strengthen food processing infrastructure
- Improve product quality and food safety
- Promote branding and marketing
- Support capacity building and training
- Encourage development of local food processing enterprises

## Major Areas of Support
Depending on the applicable component and guidelines, PMFME support may include:

- Upgradation of individual micro food processing units
- Common infrastructure
- Branding and marketing support
- Capacity building and training
- Support for Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)
- Support for Self Help Groups (SHGs)
- Support for cooperatives
- Technical assistance
- Food safety and quality improvement

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Depending on the applicable component, beneficiaries may include:

- Individual micro food processing enterprises
- Self Help Groups (SHGs)
- Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)
- Cooperatives
- Producer cooperatives
- Other eligible groups and organisations

Eligibility depends on the applicable component and current scheme guidelines.

## Benefits
Depending on the component and eligibility, PMFME may provide:

- Credit-linked capital subsidy for eligible individual micro food processing units
- Support for common infrastructure
- Branding and marketing support
- Capacity building and training
- Technical support
- Support for food safety and quality improvement
- Assistance for formalisation and business development

The exact assistance depends on the applicable component and current guidelines.

## One District One Product (ODOP)
PMFME promotes the One District One Product (ODOP) approach for developing and promoting food products associated with particular districts.

The ODOP approach can help:

- Identify suitable food products
- Promote local products
- Develop processing and value addition
- Improve branding and marketing
- Strengthen local supply chains
- Support local food processing enterprises

## Support for Individual Micro Food Processing Units
Eligible individual micro food processing units may receive credit-linked capital subsidy support for upgradation.

The assistance is subject to:

- Eligibility conditions
- Applicable scheme guidelines
- Project requirements
- Bank or lending institution appraisal
- Applicable subsidy and contribution conditions

## Support for SHGs
Self Help Groups engaged in food processing activities may receive support under applicable PMFME components.

Support may include:

- Seed capital support
- Capacity building
- Training
- Technical assistance
- Support for food processing activities

The exact assistance depends on current guidelines and eligibility.

## Support for FPOs and Cooperatives
Eligible FPOs and cooperatives involved in food processing may receive support under applicable PMFME components.

Support can include:

- Common infrastructure
- Processing facilities
- Branding and marketing
- Capacity building
- Technical assistance
- Value-chain development

## Application / Access
Eligible applicants can access PMFME support through the applicable State-level implementation mechanism and designated authorities.

Individual applicants may also need to approach the concerned bank or lending institution where credit-linked support is applicable.

The application process depends on the beneficiary category and component.

## Documents / Information
Applicants may be required to provide:

- Applicant details
- Identity and address information
- Enterprise or business details
- Bank account details
- Project details
- Food processing activity details
- Cost estimates
- Business or project proposal
- Land or premises details, where applicable
- Other documents required by the implementing authority or lending institution

Exact document requirements may vary according to the component and State.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is PMFME?
PMFME stands for Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises. It is a government scheme supporting the formalisation and upgradation of micro food processing enterprises.

### What is the main purpose of PMFME?
The main purpose is to support and formalise micro food processing enterprises while improving their infrastructure, technology, finance, quality and market access.

### Who implements PMFME?
The scheme is under the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) and is implemented through the applicable State-level agencies and other designated institutions.

### Who can benefit from PMFME?
Eligible individual micro food processing enterprises, SHGs, FPOs, cooperatives and other approved beneficiaries can receive support under applicable components.

### Does PMFME support individual food processing units?
Yes. Eligible individual micro food processing units can receive support for upgradation under applicable scheme provisions.

### Does PMFME provide subsidy?
Eligible individual units may receive credit-linked capital subsidy subject to applicable scheme guidelines and conditions.

### What is ODOP under PMFME?
ODOP means One District One Product. It promotes selected food products associated with particular districts to encourage processing, value addition, branding and marketing.

### Does PMFME support SHGs?
Yes. Eligible SHGs engaged in food processing activities can receive support under applicable PMFME components.

### Does PMFME support FPOs?
Yes. Eligible FPOs involved in food processing can receive support under applicable components.

### Does PMFME support cooperatives?
Eligible cooperatives involved in food processing may receive support under applicable PMFME components.

### Does PMFME support branding and marketing?
Yes. Branding and marketing support is available under applicable PMFME components and guidelines.

### Does PMFME support common infrastructure?
Yes. Eligible common infrastructure projects can receive support under applicable scheme provisions.

### Where can an applicant apply for PMFME?
Applicants should use the applicable State-level implementation mechanism and designated authorities. For credit-linked assistance, the concerned bank or lending institution may also be involved.

### Is PMFME available for every food processing business?
No. Eligibility depends on the beneficiary category, activity, component and applicable scheme guidelines.
