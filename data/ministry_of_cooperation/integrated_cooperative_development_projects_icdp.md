# Integrated Cooperative Development Projects (ICDP)

## Scheme ID
32

## Official Name
Integrated Cooperative Development Projects (ICDP)

## Type
Cooperative Development Programme / Project

## Ministry / Department
Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India

## Purpose
Integrated Cooperative Development Projects (ICDP) are designed to strengthen cooperative institutions and support the integrated development of cooperative activities in identified areas.

The projects aim to improve cooperative infrastructure, strengthen cooperative societies and promote economic activities through cooperatives.

## Key Objectives
- Strengthen cooperative societies
- Improve cooperative infrastructure
- Support integrated cooperative development
- Improve the capacity of cooperative institutions
- Promote economic activities through cooperatives
- Improve services provided by cooperative societies
- Support rural and agricultural cooperative development
- Encourage coordinated development of cooperative institutions

## Major Focus Areas
Depending on the approved project, ICDP activities may include:
- Cooperative infrastructure
- Storage facilities
- Processing facilities
- Marketing facilities
- Input supply
- Cooperative service centres
- Capacity building
- Training
- Institutional strengthening
- Other approved cooperative development activities

The exact activities depend on the project plan and applicable government provisions.

## Beneficiaries
Potential beneficiaries may include:
- Primary cooperative societies
- Agricultural cooperatives
- Cooperative federations
- Cooperative members
- Farmers associated with cooperatives
- Rural communities served by cooperative institutions

Eligibility and beneficiaries depend on the specific project and applicable arrangements.

## Benefits / Support
Depending on the approved project, support may include:
- Infrastructure development
- Financial assistance for approved activities
- Capacity building
- Training
- Institutional strengthening
- Improved cooperative services
- Support for agricultural and rural economic activities

The exact form and amount of assistance depend on the approved project and applicable guidelines.

## Integrated Development Approach
ICDP follows an integrated approach in which multiple cooperative development needs can be addressed together.

A project may combine infrastructure, capacity building, service improvement and economic activities according to local cooperative requirements.

## Cooperative Infrastructure
Projects may support infrastructure required for cooperative operations, including facilities for:
- Storage
- Processing
- Marketing
- Input distribution
- Collection
- Other approved cooperative services

## Agricultural and Rural Development
ICDP can contribute to rural and agricultural development by strengthening cooperative institutions that provide services to farmers and rural communities.

Support may improve access to cooperative services, markets, inputs, storage and other facilities.

## Implementation
ICDP projects are implemented through government and cooperative institutions according to the applicable project arrangements.

Implementation may involve:
- Ministry of Cooperation
- State Governments
- State cooperative departments
- Cooperative institutions
- District-level authorities
- Other designated implementing agencies

## Project Selection
ICDP projects are undertaken in identified areas according to applicable government planning and approval processes.

Project selection may consider:
- Cooperative development needs
- Infrastructure gaps
- Economic potential
- Local agricultural activities
- Existing cooperative institutions
- Development priorities
- Availability of resources

## Application / Access
ICDP is primarily a cooperative development project framework rather than a direct individual subsidy application scheme.

Eligible cooperative institutions may receive support through approved projects and designated implementing authorities.

For information about an ICDP project, cooperative organizations should contact:
- State cooperative department
- District cooperative authority
- Concerned implementing agency
- Ministry of Cooperation
- Relevant cooperative federation

## Documents / Information
Depending on the project, cooperative institutions may be required to provide:
- Registration details
- Cooperative society records
- Project proposal
- Financial information
- Details of existing infrastructure
- Proposed development activities
- Beneficiary information
- Bank account details
- Other documents required by the implementing authority

## Capacity Building
ICDP projects may include training and capacity-building activities for cooperative institutions and their members.

These activities can help improve:
- Cooperative management
- Financial management
- Technical skills
- Service delivery
- Institutional capacity

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is ICDP?
ICDP stands for Integrated Cooperative Development Projects. It is a cooperative development framework used to support integrated development of cooperative institutions and activities in identified areas.

### What does ICDP stand for?
ICDP stands for Integrated Cooperative Development Projects.

### What is the main purpose of ICDP?
Its main purpose is to strengthen cooperative institutions and support infrastructure, capacity building and economic activities through integrated cooperative development projects.

### Who can benefit from ICDP?
Eligible cooperative societies, cooperative institutions, cooperative members and communities served by the cooperatives may benefit according to the approved project.

### What type of support can ICDP provide?
Depending on the project, support may include infrastructure development, financial assistance for approved activities, training, capacity building and institutional strengthening.

### Does ICDP support cooperative infrastructure?
Yes. Approved ICDP projects may support infrastructure such as storage, processing, marketing and other cooperative facilities.

### Does ICDP support farmers?
Farmers may benefit indirectly or directly through eligible cooperative institutions and services supported by an approved ICDP project.

### Is ICDP an individual cash-benefit scheme?
No. ICDP is primarily a cooperative development project framework rather than a general individual cash-benefit scheme.

### Can a cooperative society participate in ICDP?
Eligible cooperative societies may participate through approved ICDP projects according to the applicable project and government arrangements.

### Where can a cooperative get information about ICDP?
A cooperative can approach the State cooperative department, district cooperative authority, relevant cooperative federation, designated implementing agency or Ministry of Cooperation.

### Is ICDP available across India?
ICDP projects are undertaken in identified areas according to applicable government planning and approval arrangements. Availability and implementation can therefore vary by project and location.

### Are ICDP benefits the same for every project?
No. The activities, support and beneficiaries depend on the approved project and applicable government provisions.

## Important Note
ICDP should not be presented as a single uniform individual subsidy scheme.

The scope, project area, eligible institutions, assistance and implementation arrangements may differ between approved projects.

SIA should use the latest official government information before providing specific figures, eligibility conditions or application procedures.
