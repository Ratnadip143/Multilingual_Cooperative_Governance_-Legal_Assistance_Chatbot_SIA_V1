# National Livestock Mission (NLM)

## Scheme ID
20

## Official Name
National Livestock Mission (NLM)

## Type
Centrally Sponsored Scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying

## Purpose
The National Livestock Mission (NLM) aims to promote sustainable development of the livestock sector in India.

It focuses on entrepreneurship development, improvement of livestock productivity, employment generation, breed development and strengthening of livestock-related activities.

## Key Objectives
- Promote livestock entrepreneurship
- Improve livestock productivity
- Encourage employment generation
- Support breed development
- Strengthen livestock production systems
- Promote scientific livestock management
- Improve availability of quality feed and fodder
- Encourage value addition and processing
- Improve income opportunities for livestock farmers

## Major Areas
Depending on the applicable component and current guidelines, NLM supports activities related to:

- Poultry entrepreneurship
- Sheep and goat entrepreneurship
- Pig entrepreneurship
- Feed and fodder development
- Livestock breed improvement
- Livestock-related infrastructure
- Entrepreneurship development
- Capacity building and training

## Entrepreneurship Development
NLM promotes entrepreneurship in livestock activities.

Eligible entrepreneurs may receive support for approved livestock enterprises such as:

- Poultry farming
- Sheep and goat farming
- Pig farming
- Feed and fodder enterprises
- Other approved livestock activities

The exact activities and assistance depend on the applicable component and current guidelines.

## Feed and Fodder Development
NLM supports development of feed and fodder resources to improve livestock production.

Activities may include:

- Fodder cultivation
- Fodder seed production
- Feed production
- Fodder storage
- Hay and silage production
- Other approved feed and fodder infrastructure

## Breed Development
The mission promotes development and improvement of livestock breeds.

It may support activities related to:

- Breed improvement
- Genetic development
- Quality breeding
- Scientific livestock management
- Conservation and development of suitable livestock breeds

## Benefits
Depending on the applicable component, support may include:

- Financial assistance for eligible projects
- Support for livestock entrepreneurship
- Development of livestock infrastructure
- Feed and fodder development
- Breed improvement
- Capacity building and training
- Employment generation
- Improved livestock productivity
- Better income opportunities

The exact amount and pattern of assistance depend on the component, beneficiary and current government guidelines.

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Depending on the applicable component, eligible beneficiaries may include:

- Individual entrepreneurs
- Livestock farmers
- Farmer groups
- Self Help Groups (SHGs)
- Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)
- Cooperatives
- Joint Liability Groups (JLGs)
- Section 8 companies
- Other eligible organisations and institutions

Eligibility varies according to the specific component and applicable guidelines.

## Support for Livestock Farmers
Livestock farmers may benefit through eligible entrepreneurship projects, breed improvement activities, feed and fodder development and related livestock services.

The availability of specific assistance depends on the State and applicable programme component.

## Application / Access
NLM is implemented through the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying and concerned State Animal Husbandry Departments and designated agencies.

Eligible applicants should use the applicable government application process or approach the concerned State Animal Husbandry Department or designated implementing authority.

The application procedure depends on the activity and beneficiary category.

## Documents / Information
Applicants may be required to provide:

- Applicant details
- Identity and address information
- Bank account details
- Livestock or project details
- Land or farm details, where applicable
- Project proposal
- Cost estimates
- Registration documents, where applicable
- Other documents required by the implementing authority

Exact document requirements may vary by component and State.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is the National Livestock Mission?
National Livestock Mission is a government programme aimed at sustainable development of the livestock sector, including entrepreneurship, productivity and breed development.

### What is the main purpose of NLM?
The main purpose is to promote livestock development, entrepreneurship, productivity, employment and better livestock production systems.

### Who can benefit from NLM?
Eligible livestock farmers, entrepreneurs, SHGs, FPOs, cooperatives and other approved beneficiaries can benefit under applicable components.

### Does NLM support livestock entrepreneurship?
Yes. Entrepreneurship development is an important area of NLM.

### Does NLM support sheep and goat farming?
Eligible sheep and goat entrepreneurship activities can receive support under applicable NLM components.

### Does NLM support pig farming?
Eligible pig entrepreneurship activities can receive support under applicable NLM components.

### Does NLM support poultry?
Eligible poultry entrepreneurship activities can receive support under applicable components.

### Does NLM support feed and fodder?
Yes. Feed and fodder development is an important area supported under NLM.

### Does NLM help improve livestock productivity?
Yes. Breed development, scientific management and improved livestock production systems are intended to improve productivity.

### Who implements NLM?
NLM is implemented through the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, State Animal Husbandry Departments and designated implementing agencies.

### Where can applicants access NLM?
Applicants should approach the concerned State Animal Husbandry Department or use the applicable government application process for the relevant component.

### Is the same financial assistance available for every applicant?
No. Assistance depends on the component, project, beneficiary category and applicable government guidelines.
