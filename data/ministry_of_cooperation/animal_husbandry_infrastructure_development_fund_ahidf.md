# Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF)

## Scheme ID
23

## Official Name
Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF)

## Type
Central Sector Scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying

## Purpose
The Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF) aims to encourage investment in infrastructure for the animal husbandry and dairy sectors.

It supports development and modernization of eligible infrastructure for dairy processing, meat processing, animal feed, breed multiplication and other approved activities.

## Key Objectives
- Encourage investment in animal husbandry infrastructure
- Strengthen dairy processing infrastructure
- Support meat processing infrastructure
- Promote animal feed manufacturing
- Improve livestock productivity
- Encourage private investment in the sector
- Promote entrepreneurship and employment
- Improve value addition in livestock products
- Strengthen supply chains and related infrastructure

## Major Areas
Depending on the applicable component and current guidelines, AHIDF supports eligible projects related to:

- Dairy processing
- Dairy product manufacturing
- Meat processing
- Meat product manufacturing
- Animal feed manufacturing
- Breed multiplication farms
- Breed improvement infrastructure
- Veterinary vaccine and drug manufacturing
- Other approved animal husbandry infrastructure

## Dairy Processing
AHIDF supports eligible investment in dairy processing infrastructure.

Projects may include:

- Milk processing plants
- Dairy product manufacturing facilities
- Milk packaging infrastructure
- Value-added dairy product facilities
- Cold-chain infrastructure
- Modernization of existing dairy plants

## Meat Processing
Eligible projects may include infrastructure for:

- Meat processing
- Meat product manufacturing
- Modern slaughtering facilities
- Cold-chain facilities
- Storage and transportation
- Value addition and processing

Projects must comply with applicable government standards and regulations.

## Animal Feed Infrastructure
AHIDF supports eligible animal feed manufacturing infrastructure.

Activities may include:

- Cattle feed plants
- Poultry feed plants
- Animal feed processing
- Feed storage
- Feed manufacturing equipment
- Other approved feed-related infrastructure

## Breed Multiplication
The fund can support eligible breed multiplication and related livestock infrastructure.

Such projects may contribute to:

- Improved availability of quality livestock
- Breed development
- Genetic improvement
- Livestock productivity
- Scientific livestock management

## Benefits
Depending on the eligible project and applicable guidelines, AHIDF may provide:

- Interest subvention on eligible loans
- Credit support for approved projects
- Support for infrastructure development
- Assistance for dairy processing
- Support for meat processing
- Support for animal feed manufacturing
- Support for breed multiplication
- Encouragement for private investment
- Employment generation
- Value addition in livestock products

The exact financial assistance, loan conditions and eligible project categories depend on the current government guidelines.

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Depending on the applicable guidelines, eligible entities may include:

- Individual entrepreneurs
- Private companies
- Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)
- Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)
- Dairy cooperatives
- Milk producer companies
- Section 8 companies
- Other eligible private or cooperative organisations

Eligibility depends on the project category and current guidelines.

## Support for Entrepreneurs
AHIDF is designed to encourage investment and entrepreneurship in animal husbandry infrastructure.

Eligible entrepreneurs may establish or expand approved infrastructure projects in dairy processing, meat processing, animal feed, breed multiplication and other supported areas.

## Financing
AHIDF provides financial support through eligible lending institutions for approved projects.

The government framework may provide interest subvention or other financing support for eligible loans.

SIA should not state a fixed current interest rate, loan amount or assistance percentage unless it is confirmed from the latest official guidelines.

## Application / Access
Eligible applicants should follow the current AHIDF application process specified by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying.

Applications and project proposals may be processed through the designated online platform and eligible lending institutions, depending on the applicable guidelines.

Applicants should verify the current application procedure before applying.

Applicants may be required to provide:

- Project proposal
- Detailed Project Report (DPR)
- Project cost estimates

- Bank or financing details

- Other documents required by the lending or implementing authority

Exact requirements vary according to the project and financing institution.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is AHIDF?
AHIDF stands for Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund. It supports eligible infrastructure projects in the animal husbandry, dairy and related sectors.

### What is the main purpose of AHIDF?
Its main purpose is to encourage investment and development of infrastructure for dairy processing, meat processing, animal feed, breed multiplication and other approved activities.

### Who can benefit from AHIDF?
Eligible entrepreneurs, private companies, MSMEs, FPOs, dairy cooperatives and other approved organisations can seek support for eligible projects.

### Does AHIDF support dairy processing?
Yes. Eligible dairy processing and value-addition infrastructure can receive support.

### Does AHIDF support meat processing?
Yes. Eligible meat processing and related infrastructure can be supported under applicable guidelines.

### Does AHIDF support animal feed plants?
Yes. Eligible animal feed manufacturing infrastructure is an important area of support.

### Does AHIDF support breed multiplication?
Yes. Eligible breed multiplication and related livestock infrastructure can receive support.

### Does AHIDF support cooperatives?
Eligible dairy cooperatives and other approved cooperative organisations may receive support for eligible projects.

### Does AHIDF provide loans?
AHIDF provides financing support through eligible lending institutions for approved projects, subject to applicable conditions.

### Does AHIDF provide interest support?
Eligible projects may receive interest subvention or other financing support according to the current government framework.

### Can an individual entrepreneur apply?
Eligible individual entrepreneurs can apply for approved projects subject to the eligibility and financing conditions applicable at the time of application.

### Where can applicants apply?
Applicants should follow the current application process prescribed by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying and designated lending or implementing institutions.

### Is the same assistance available for every project?
No. Assistance depends on the project category, applicant, loan structure and current government guidelines.
