# Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission (SPMRM)

## Scheme ID
30

## Official Name
Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission (SPMRM)

## Type
Centrally Sponsored Scheme

## Ministry / Department
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India

## Purpose
The Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission (SPMRM) aims to develop rural areas by providing better economic, social and physical infrastructure while preserving the rural character of the areas.

The mission focuses on developing clusters of villages with improved infrastructure, services and economic opportunities.

## Key Objectives
- Develop rural areas through planned cluster development
- Provide better economic opportunities
- Improve social infrastructure
- Improve physical infrastructure
- Provide better access to essential services
- Strengthen rural livelihoods
- Promote balanced rural development
- Reduce the rural-urban development gap
- Preserve the rural character of village clusters

## Major Features
SPMRM follows a cluster-based approach to rural development.

A group of geographically connected villages is developed as a cluster and provided with infrastructure and services intended to improve the quality of life and economic opportunities of rural residents.

The mission combines resources from different government programmes and encourages convergence of schemes.

## Rurban Clusters
A Rurban cluster consists of a group of geographically contiguous villages with population and socio-economic characteristics that make them suitable for coordinated development.

Clusters are selected according to applicable government criteria and planning processes.

## Infrastructure and Services
Depending on the approved Integrated Cluster Action Plan and local requirements, development may cover areas such as:
- Roads and transport facilities
- Sanitation
- Drinking water
- Solid and liquid waste management
- Electricity and street lighting
- Digital connectivity
- Skill development
- Economic activities
- Livelihood opportunities
- Health facilities
- Education facilities
- Social infrastructure

The exact facilities depend on the approved cluster development plan.

## Convergence
SPMRM promotes convergence of resources from different government schemes and programmes.

The objective is to coordinate investments so that multiple development needs of a rural cluster can be addressed together.

## Integrated Cluster Action Plan
Development of a Rurban cluster is guided by an Integrated Cluster Action Plan.

The plan identifies:
- Infrastructure requirements
- Economic development opportunities
- Social development needs
- Basic service gaps
- Livelihood opportunities
- Convergence requirements

The plan is prepared according to the applicable mission framework and local development priorities.

## Benefits
SPMRM can support rural residents through improved:
- Infrastructure
- Basic services
- Connectivity
- Livelihood opportunities
- Skill development
- Economic activities
- Social facilities
- Quality of life

Benefits are delivered through cluster-level development rather than as a direct individual cash benefit.

## Beneficiaries
The mission primarily benefits residents of selected Rurban clusters.

Potential beneficiaries include:
- Rural households
- Farmers
- Rural workers
- Women
- Youth
- Local entrepreneurs
- Self-help groups
- Other residents of selected village clusters

## Selection of Clusters
Rurban clusters are selected according to government criteria and planning processes.

Selection may consider factors such as:
- Population
- Geographic contiguity
- Socio-economic conditions
- Growth potential
- Existing infrastructure
- Economic opportunities
- Development needs

## Implementation
SPMRM is implemented through coordination between:
- Ministry of Rural Development
- State Governments
- State-level mission authorities
- District administrations
- Local bodies
- Other implementing agencies

Implementation arrangements may vary according to the applicable government framework.

## Application / Access
SPMRM is primarily a cluster-level development programme rather than an individual beneficiary application scheme.

A rural resident generally benefits through development activities implemented in a selected Rurban cluster.

For information about a particular cluster or development activity, residents can approach:
- Gram Panchayat
- Local body
- Block Development Office
- District administration
- State rural development authorities

## Documents / Information
Individual residents normally do not apply directly for the mission as a personal subsidy scheme.

For cluster planning and implementation, authorities may use information related to:
- Population
- Infrastructure
- Basic services
- Local economic activities
- Livelihoods
- Development gaps
- Geographic and socio-economic conditions

## Economic Development
SPMRM seeks to improve economic opportunities in rural clusters through activities such as:
- Skill development
- Local enterprise promotion
- Livelihood support
- Agricultural and allied activities
- Rural business opportunities
- Improved connectivity and infrastructure

The exact activities depend on local cluster plans.

## Rural-Urban Gap
The mission seeks to reduce the development gap between rural and urban areas by providing rural clusters with better infrastructure, services and economic opportunities while retaining their rural character.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is SPMRM?
SPMRM stands for Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission. It is a rural development mission that develops selected clusters of villages through improved infrastructure, services and economic opportunities.

### What does SPMRM stand for?
SPMRM stands for Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission.

### What is the main purpose of SPMRM?
Its main purpose is to develop rural clusters by improving infrastructure, basic services and economic opportunities while preserving the rural character of the area.

### Who benefits from SPMRM?
Residents of selected Rurban clusters can benefit from improved infrastructure, services, connectivity and economic opportunities.

### Is SPMRM a cash benefit scheme?
No. SPMRM is primarily a cluster-level rural development programme rather than an individual cash-benefit scheme.

### What is a Rurban cluster?
A Rurban cluster is a group of geographically connected villages selected for coordinated development under the mission.

### What type of infrastructure can SPMRM support?
Depending on the approved cluster plan, infrastructure may include roads, drinking water, sanitation, electricity, digital connectivity, health, education and other essential services.

### Does SPMRM support employment and livelihoods?
Yes. The mission can support skill development, livelihood opportunities and local economic activities as part of cluster development.

### What is an Integrated Cluster Action Plan?
It is a development plan that identifies the infrastructure, economic and social needs of a selected Rurban cluster and coordinates the required interventions.

### Can an individual directly apply for SPMRM?
Generally, no. SPMRM operates mainly through selected village clusters and government development plans rather than individual subsidy applications.

### Where can a rural resident get information about SPMRM?
Residents can approach their Gram Panchayat, Block Development Office, district administration or State rural development authority for information about activities in their cluster.

### Is SPMRM available in every village?
No. The mission works through selected Rurban clusters according to applicable government selection and planning criteria.

## Important Note
SPMRM is a cluster-based rural development programme. It should not be presented as a single individual financial assistance scheme.

The facilities, development activities and implementation arrangements may differ between clusters according to approved plans and applicable government guidelines.

SIA should use the latest official government information when answering questions about current cluster selection, funding or implementation status.
