MINISTRY OF COOPERATION — GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

The Ministry of Cooperation was created by the Union Government on 6 July 2021.

The Ministry is responsible for providing a separate administrative, legal and policy framework for strengthening the cooperative movement in the country.

Its objectives include:
- Strengthening the cooperative movement and deepening its reach to the grassroots.
- Promoting a cooperative-based economic development model.
- Creating an appropriate policy, legal and institutional framework for cooperatives.
- Promoting transparency, modernization and computerization of cooperatives.
- Supporting the development of Multi-State Cooperative Societies.
- Connecting villages with cooperatives and promoting rural development.

MULTI-STATE COOPERATIVE SOCIETIES

The Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002 provides the legal framework for cooperative societies whose objects are not confined to one State and which serve the interests of members in more than one State.

The Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies is responsible for registration and other regulatory processes relating to Multi-State Cooperative Societies.

COOPERATIVE PRINCIPLES

The universally accepted cooperative principles include:
1. Open and voluntary membership.
2. Democratic participation of members.
3. Members' economic participation.
4. Autonomy and independence.
5. Education, training and information.
6. Cooperation among cooperatives.
7. Concern for the community.
