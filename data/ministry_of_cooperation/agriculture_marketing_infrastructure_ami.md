# Agriculture Marketing Infrastructure (AMI)

## Scheme ID
12

## Official Name
Agriculture Marketing Infrastructure (AMI)

## Type
Sub-scheme of Integrated Scheme for Agricultural Marketing (ISAM)

## Ministry / Organisation
Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

## Implementing / Channelising Agency
- Directorate of Marketing & Inspection (DMI)
- National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) for eligible subsidy channelisation

## Purpose
The Agriculture Marketing Infrastructure (AMI) scheme aims to strengthen agricultural marketing infrastructure and improve the storage and handling of agricultural produce.

The scheme supports the construction and renovation of godowns and warehouses in rural areas and helps increase storage capacity for agricultural produce.

## Key Objectives
- Create scientific storage infrastructure
- Increase storage capacity for agricultural produce
- Reduce post-harvest losses
- Improve agricultural marketing infrastructure
- Support better handling and storage of farm produce
- Help farmers obtain better marketing opportunities
- Strengthen the agricultural supply chain

## Major Components
AMI supports eligible agricultural marketing infrastructure, including:

- Godowns
- Warehouses
- Storage infrastructure
- Other eligible agricultural marketing infrastructure
- Infrastructure for handling and managing agricultural produce

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Eligible beneficiaries may include:

- Individual farmers
- Groups of farmers / growers
- Agri-preneurs
- Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
- Cooperatives
- Companies
- State agencies
- Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs)
- Other eligible institutions as specified under the applicable guidelines

## Financial Assistance
AMI provides capital investment subsidy for eligible projects.

The subsidy is generally provided at:

- 25% of eligible project cost for applicable beneficiary categories
- 33.33% for specified special categories of beneficiaries

The exact subsidy rate, ceiling and eligibility conditions depend on the applicable AMI guidelines and beneficiary category.

## Benefits
- Development of rural storage infrastructure
- Increased storage capacity
- Reduction in post-harvest losses
- Better handling of agricultural produce
- Improved agricultural marketing facilities
- Support for scientific storage
- Better opportunities for farmers and producer organisations

## Application / Access
Eligible applicants can approach eligible financial institutions and follow the prescribed application and project approval process.

For projects eligible for subsidy through NABARD, the subsidy is channelised according to the applicable scheme guidelines.

The exact application process and financial conditions should be verified from the latest official AMI guidelines.

## Important Note
AMI is a demand-driven scheme and assistance depends on the eligibility of the beneficiary and project under the applicable guidelines.

Subsidy rates, eligible activities, project ceilings and other conditions may change.

SIA should use the latest official Government of India / Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare information when answering questions about current AMI conditions.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is the Agriculture Marketing Infrastructure scheme?
AMI is a sub-scheme of the Integrated Scheme for Agricultural Marketing that supports the creation and improvement of agricultural marketing and storage infrastructure.

### What is the main purpose of AMI?
The main purpose is to strengthen agricultural marketing infrastructure and increase storage capacity for agricultural produce.

### What infrastructure is supported under AMI?
AMI supports eligible godowns, warehouses, storage infrastructure and other agricultural marketing infrastructure.

### Who can benefit from AMI?
Farmers, groups of farmers, agri-preneurs, FPOs, cooperatives, state agencies and other eligible entities can benefit subject to applicable guidelines.

### Can FPOs benefit from AMI?
Yes. Eligible Farmer Producer Organizations can receive assistance subject to the applicable scheme conditions.

### Can cooperatives benefit from AMI?
Yes. Eligible cooperative organisations can receive assistance for eligible projects under the scheme.

### What is the subsidy under AMI?
The subsidy is generally 25% or 33.33% of eligible project cost depending on the category of beneficiary and applicable scheme conditions.

### Does AMI help farmers with storage?
Yes. The scheme supports development of storage infrastructure that can improve the availability of storage facilities for agricultural produce.

### Does AMI reduce post-harvest losses?
The development of scientific storage and marketing infrastructure is intended to help reduce post-harvest and handling losses.

### Is AMI part of ISAM?
Yes. Agriculture Marketing Infrastructure is a sub-scheme of the Integrated Scheme for Agricultural Marketing (ISAM).
