# Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)

## Scheme ID
13

## Official Name
Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)

## Type
Central Sector / Centrally Sponsored agricultural mechanization scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

## Purpose
The Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM) aims to promote agricultural mechanization and improve access to modern farm machinery and equipment.

The scheme supports farmers in adopting appropriate agricultural machinery to improve farm productivity, reduce cultivation costs and address labour-related challenges.

## Key Objectives
- Promote agricultural mechanization
- Improve access to modern farm machinery
- Increase farm productivity
- Reduce the cost and time involved in agricultural operations
- Address shortages of agricultural labour
- Promote efficient use of agricultural machinery
- Improve access to machinery for small and marginal farmers

## Major Components

### Financial Assistance
Eligible farmers and other approved beneficiaries can receive financial assistance for the purchase of eligible agricultural machinery and equipment according to applicable guidelines.

### Custom Hiring Centres
The scheme supports establishment and strengthening of Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) so that farmers can access farm machinery without having to purchase expensive equipment individually.

### Farm Machinery Banks
Farm Machinery Banks can help groups of farmers and rural organisations access agricultural machinery collectively.

### Demonstration and Training
The scheme also supports demonstrations, training and awareness activities related to agricultural machinery and its proper use.

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Depending on the applicable guidelines and component, beneficiaries may include:

- Individual farmers
- Small and marginal farmers
- Farmer groups
- Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
- Self Help Groups (SHGs)
- Cooperatives
- Rural entrepreneurs
- Custom Hiring Centre operators
- Other eligible agricultural organisations

## Benefits
- Financial support for eligible agricultural machinery
- Better access to modern farm equipment
- Reduced dependence on manual labour
- Reduction in cultivation time
- Improved efficiency of farm operations
- Access to machinery through Custom Hiring Centres
- Support for collective use of agricultural machinery

## Agricultural Machinery
Eligible machinery and equipment may include machines used for activities such as:

- Land preparation
- Sowing and planting
- Crop protection
- Harvesting
- Threshing
- Residue management
- Post-harvest operations

The exact machinery eligible for assistance depends on the applicable component and current government guidelines.

## Custom Hiring Centres

Custom Hiring Centres provide farmers access to agricultural machinery on a hiring basis.

This is particularly useful for small and marginal farmers who may not be able to purchase expensive agricultural machinery themselves.

## Farm Machinery Banks

Farm Machinery Banks support collective access to agricultural machinery by eligible groups and organisations.

They can help improve machinery availability in rural areas and promote shared use of farm equipment.

## Application / Access
Eligible applicants can apply through the applicable State Government agriculture department / designated implementing agency or the official agricultural mechanization platform, according to the current implementation process.

The application procedure, documents, machinery list and assistance available may vary by component and State.

## Documents / Information
Applicants may be required to provide:

- Farmer / beneficiary details
- Identity and address information
- Land or agricultural activity details, where applicable
- Bank account details
- Machinery or project details
- Other documents required by the implementing agency

Exact requirements depend on the component and State-level implementation.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is SMAM?
SMAM stands for Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization. It is a government initiative to promote agricultural mechanization and improve farmers' access to modern machinery.

### What is the main purpose of SMAM?
The main purpose is to promote the use of agricultural machinery, improve farm efficiency and make modern farm equipment more accessible to farmers.

### Who can benefit from SMAM?
Eligible farmers, farmer groups, FPOs, SHGs, cooperatives and other approved beneficiaries can benefit according to applicable guidelines.

### Does SMAM provide financial assistance for farm machinery?
Yes. Eligible beneficiaries can receive financial assistance for specified agricultural machinery and equipment subject to applicable conditions.

### What is a Custom Hiring Centre?
A Custom Hiring Centre provides agricultural machinery to farmers on a hiring basis, allowing them to use equipment without purchasing it.

### Can small farmers benefit from Custom Hiring Centres?
Yes. Custom Hiring Centres can help small and marginal farmers access machinery that may otherwise be expensive to purchase.

### What is a Farm Machinery Bank?
A Farm Machinery Bank is a mechanism for providing access to agricultural machinery collectively through eligible groups or organisations.

### Can FPOs benefit from SMAM?
Eligible FPOs may benefit from applicable components of the scheme subject to current guidelines.

### Can cooperatives benefit from SMAM?
Eligible cooperatives may participate in applicable components subject to the prescribed conditions.

### Where can farmers apply for SMAM?
Farmers can apply through the designated State agriculture department / implementing agency or the applicable official agricultural mechanization platform.

### Does SMAM cover all agricultural machinery?
No. Only machinery and equipment covered under the applicable scheme components and current guidelines are eligible.
