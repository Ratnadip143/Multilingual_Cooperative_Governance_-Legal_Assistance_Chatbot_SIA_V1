# Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)

## Basic Information

**Official Name:** Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS)

**Common Name:** PACS

**Type:** Grassroots cooperative credit society

**Sector:** Agriculture, rural credit and cooperative development

**Primary Role:** PACS are the grassroots level institutions of the Short-Term Cooperative Credit Structure (STCC). They deal directly with rural and agricultural borrowers and provide credit and other agricultural and cooperative services.

**Official Authority:** Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India

Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) are village- or panchayat-level cooperative societies that form the lowest tier of the Short-Term Cooperative Credit Structure.

PACS deal directly with rural and agricultural borrowers. They provide loans, collect repayment of loans, and may undertake distribution and marketing functions.

PACS are an important link between farmers and the higher levels of the cooperative credit system, including District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCBs), State Cooperative Banks (StCBs), and NABARD.

---

## Why are PACS Important?

PACS help connect farmers and rural communities with:

* Agricultural credit
* Farm inputs
* Agricultural services
* Storage facilities
* Markets and distribution
* Other cooperative and government-supported services

The Ministry of Cooperation describes PACS as the grassroots base of the cooperative credit structure.

PACS can help bring farmers closer to credit, agricultural inputs, markets and value addition.

---

## What Services Can PACS Provide?

Traditional PACS activities include:

* Short-term agricultural credit
* Medium-term credit
* Distribution of seeds
* Distribution of fertilizers
* Distribution of pesticides
* Storage-related services
* Agricultural input services
* Marketing and distribution functions
* Loan repayment and related credit services

Under the Model Bye-laws and diversification initiatives, PACS can also undertake multiple activities beyond traditional credit.

These may include:

* Dairy activities
* Fisheries activities
* Floriculture
* Food-grain procurement
* Warehousing and storage
* Fair Price Shops
* LPG/CNG/Petrol/Diesel distributorship
* Common Service Centre (CSC) services
* Business Correspondent or banking-related activities
* Insurance-related services
* Custom Hiring Centres
* Other permitted business activities

The exact activities available at an individual PACS depend on its applicable State Cooperative Societies Act, bye-laws, permissions and local implementation.

---

## What Types of Loans Does PACS Provide?

PACS traditionally provide:

* Short-term agricultural credit
* Medium-term agricultural credit

The Model Bye-laws and computerization framework also cover financial services involving short-, medium- and long-term loans.

However, the exact loan products, limits, interest rates, repayment periods and eligibility conditions depend on the applicable cooperative rules, State provisions and the individual PACS or financing structure.

SIA should not give a fixed loan amount or interest rate unless it has a verified current official source.

---

## How Does PACS Connect With NABARD and Cooperative Banks?

PACS form the grassroots level of the Short-Term Cooperative Credit Structure.

The higher levels include:

1. Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)
2. District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCBs)
3. State Cooperative Banks (StCBs)

NABARD supports and refinances the cooperative credit system through the higher cooperative banking structure.

PACS deal directly with rural and agricultural borrowers.

## Who Can Become a PACS Member?

Membership of a PACS is governed by the applicable State Cooperative Societies Act, rules and the society's bye-laws.

Therefore, there is no single universal membership rule or document list that applies identically to every PACS in India.

A person interested in becoming a PACS member should check:

* The applicable State cooperative law
* The PACS bye-laws
* The membership eligibility conditions
* The local PACS office
* Any required membership or share contribution
* Documents required by the concerned PACS

## How Can I Join a PACS?

A general PACS membership process may involve:

1. Identify the relevant PACS in the person's area.
2. Check the applicable State cooperative law and PACS bye-laws.
3. Obtain the membership application form or use the available online registration facility, where implemented.
4. Complete the required KYC and submit the required documents.
5. Submit identity, residence, land-holding or other applicable proof as required.
6. Pay the applicable admission fee and share contribution, if required.
7. The application may be placed before the Board of Directors for a decision according to the applicable bye-laws.
8. If approved, complete the remaining membership formalities.

Under the Model Bye-laws, the Board of Directors decides the membership application within the prescribed period, and the applicant is informed of the decision according to the applicable procedure.

The exact process, eligibility, documents, fees and share requirements may differ between States and individual PACS.

## Can PACS Give Loans to Farmers?

Yes. PACS directly deal with rural and agricultural borrowers and provide credit.

PACS traditionally provide short-term and medium-term agricultural credit to member farmers.

Loan availability, amount, interest rate, repayment period, collateral requirements and eligibility depend on the applicable rules and the concerned cooperative credit institution.

SIA should verify current loan conditions before giving specific financial figures.

---

## What Agricultural Inputs Can PACS Provide?

PACS may provide or distribute agricultural inputs such as:

* Seeds
* Fertilizers
* Pesticides

They may also provide other agricultural services depending on their activities and applicable rules.

---

## Can PACS Provide Storage Services?

Yes.

PACS can undertake storage and warehousing-related activities.

The Model Bye-laws support diversification of PACS into activities such as godowns, warehousing and food-grain procurement.

Individual availability depends on the PACS, local implementation and applicable permissions.

---

## Can PACS Function as Common Service Centres?

Yes.

The Ministry of Cooperation has enabled PACS to function as Common Service Centres (CSCs).

Through the CSC framework, PACS can provide various e-governance and citizen-centric services.

These can include services related to:

* Banking
* Insurance
* Aadhaar enrolment or updation
* Health services
* Agricultural services
* Government welfare schemes
* Other permitted digital services

The exact services available at a particular PACS may vary.

---

## Can PACS Become Multipurpose Cooperative Societies?

Yes.

The Ministry of Cooperation has prepared Model Bye-laws to help PACS diversify their activities and become multipurpose cooperative societies.

The Model Bye-laws enable PACS to undertake more than 25 types of business activities across areas such as agriculture, dairy, fisheries, storage, retail and citizen services.

Adoption and implementation are subject to the applicable State Cooperative Societies Act and State-level requirements.

---

## PACS Computerization

The Government of India approved the Centrally Sponsored Project for Computerization of PACS in June 2022.

The project aims to bring functional PACS onto an ERP-based common software system and connect them with NABARD through State Cooperative Banks and District Central Cooperative Banks.

The computerization initiative aims to:

* Improve operational efficiency
* Speed up loan disbursal
* Reduce transaction costs
* Improve accounting
* Increase transparency
* Improve coordination with DCCBs and StCBs
