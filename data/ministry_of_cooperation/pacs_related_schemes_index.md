# Official PACS Related Schemes

## Purpose

This page provides the official list of schemes and initiatives related to Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS).

## Official Source

Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India

## Official PACS Related Schemes List

The official list of PACS-related schemes can be checked on the Ministry of Cooperation website:

https://www.cooperation.gov.in/en/pacs-related-schemes

## Important Note

SIA should use the official Ministry of Cooperation PACS-related schemes page for the current list rather than assuming that every scheme document in the knowledge base represents an active scheme.

Scheme availability and status may change. The official Ministry source should therefore be treated as the authoritative source for the current PACS-related scheme list.
