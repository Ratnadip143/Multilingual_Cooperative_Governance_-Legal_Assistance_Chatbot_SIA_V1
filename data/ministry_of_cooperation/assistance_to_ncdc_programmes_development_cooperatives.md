# Assistance to NCDC Programmes for Development of Cooperatives

## Scheme ID
33

## Official Name
Assistance to NCDC Programmes for Development of Cooperatives

## Type
Central Sector Cooperative Development Assistance

## Ministry / Department
Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India

## Implementing Agency
National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC)

## Purpose
The programme provides assistance through the National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC) for the development and strengthening of cooperative societies and cooperative activities.

It supports eligible cooperative institutions in undertaking approved development activities and improving their economic and service capabilities.

## Key Objectives
- Promote development of cooperative societies
- Strengthen cooperative institutions
- Support cooperative economic activities
- Improve cooperative infrastructure
- Facilitate access to financial assistance for approved cooperative projects
- Support agricultural and allied cooperative activities
- Improve production, processing, storage and marketing facilities
- Strengthen cooperative-based rural development

## Major Areas of Support
Depending on the approved programme and project, NCDC assistance may cover areas such as:
- Agriculture and allied activities
- Dairy
- Fisheries
- Handloom and other cooperative activities
- Processing
- Storage
- Marketing
- Infrastructure development
- Working capital requirements
- Cooperative service facilities

The exact activities and assistance depend on applicable NCDC programmes and guidelines.

## Beneficiaries
Potential beneficiaries may include eligible:
- Cooperative societies
- Cooperative federations
- Cooperative institutions
- Producer cooperatives
- Agricultural cooperatives
- Dairy cooperatives
- Fisheries cooperatives
- Other eligible cooperative organizations

Eligibility depends on the type of project and applicable NCDC provisions.

## Benefits / Financial Assistance
NCDC may provide financial assistance for eligible cooperative development projects.

Depending on the programme and project, assistance may take forms such as:
- Loans
- Investment support
- Subsidy-linked assistance where applicable
- Financial assistance for infrastructure
- Support for approved development activities

The exact assistance, financial terms and conditions depend on the applicable NCDC programme and current guidelines.

## Infrastructure Development
Assistance may support infrastructure required for cooperative activities, including:
- Storage facilities
- Processing units
- Marketing infrastructure
- Dairy infrastructure
- Fisheries infrastructure
- Collection facilities
- Other approved cooperative infrastructure

## Agriculture and Allied Activities
NCDC programmes can support cooperative activities related to:
- Agriculture
- Dairy
- Fisheries
- Horticulture
- Livestock
- Agricultural inputs
- Processing
- Marketing
- Storage

The availability of support depends on the specific programme and eligibility conditions.

## Cooperative Development
The programme contributes to cooperative development by helping eligible cooperative institutions improve their infrastructure, financial capacity, production activities and services to members.

## Application / Access
Eligible cooperative organizations generally approach NCDC or the concerned State/Union Territory cooperative authorities through the applicable project and programme process.

The process may involve:
- Identification of an eligible project
- Preparation of a project proposal
- Submission through the designated channel
- Technical and financial appraisal
- Approval according to applicable rules
- Release of assistance subject to conditions

The exact process varies according to the NCDC programme.

## Documents / Information
Depending on the project, applicants may be required to provide:
- Registration certificate of the cooperative
- Cooperative society records
- Project proposal
- Detailed project report where required
- Financial statements
- Details of existing activities
- Estimated project cost
- Means of financing
- Bank details
- Land or infrastructure details where applicable
- Other documents required by NCDC or the concerned authority

Exact requirements depend on the specific programme.

## Role of NCDC
The National Cooperative Development Corporation is a statutory corporation that supports cooperative development through financial assistance and other approved programmes.

NCDC works with cooperative institutions and relevant government authorities to support eligible development projects.

## Implementation
Implementation may involve:
- Ministry of Cooperation
- National Cooperative Development Corporation
- State Governments
- Union Territory administrations
- State cooperative departments
- Eligible cooperative institutions
- Other designated agencies

## Project Appraisal
Projects seeking NCDC assistance may be examined for:
- Eligibility
- Technical feasibility
- Financial viability
- Project cost
- Repayment capability where applicable
- Cooperative benefits
- Compliance with applicable guidelines

Approval is subject to the relevant programme and NCDC procedures.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is Assistance to NCDC Programmes for Development of Cooperatives?
It is a cooperative development assistance programme through NCDC that supports eligible cooperative institutions and approved cooperative development activities.

### What does NCDC stand for?
NCDC stands for National Cooperative Development Corporation.

### What is the main purpose of NCDC assistance?
The main purpose is to support the development and strengthening of eligible cooperative institutions and their approved economic and infrastructure activities.

### Who can receive NCDC assistance?
Eligible cooperative societies, federations and other cooperative institutions may receive assistance subject to the applicable programme and eligibility conditions.

### What type of assistance does NCDC provide?
Depending on the programme, NCDC may provide loans, financial assistance and other approved forms of support for cooperative development projects.

### Does NCDC support cooperative infrastructure?
Yes. Eligible infrastructure projects related to cooperative activities may receive assistance under applicable NCDC programmes.

### Does NCDC support dairy cooperatives?
NCDC programmes may support eligible dairy cooperative activities and infrastructure according to applicable programme provisions.

### Does NCDC support fisheries cooperatives?
Eligible fisheries cooperative activities and infrastructure may receive support under applicable NCDC programmes.

### Does NCDC support agricultural cooperatives?
Yes. NCDC supports eligible agricultural cooperative activities through applicable development programmes.

### How can a cooperative obtain NCDC assistance?
An eligible cooperative should follow the applicable NCDC programme process and may approach NCDC or the concerned State/Union Territory cooperative authority for guidance.

### What documents are required for NCDC assistance?
Documents depend on the project and programme but may include cooperative registration documents, project proposals, financial statements, project cost details and other documents specified by NCDC.

### Is NCDC assistance available for every cooperative?
No. Assistance is subject to programme eligibility, project requirements, appraisal and applicable NCDC rules.

### Is NCDC assistance always a grant?
No. Depending on the programme, assistance may include loans or other forms of financial support. The applicable programme determines the nature and terms of assistance.

### Is NCDC a government organization?
Yes. NCDC is a statutory corporation established by the Government of India for cooperative development.

## Important Note
NCDC operates multiple programmes and provides different forms of assistance for different cooperative activities.

SIA should not state a fixed loan amount, subsidy percentage, interest rate or eligibility condition unless it is supported by the latest official NCDC information for the specific programme.

The application route and project requirements may vary according to the type of cooperative activity and current NCDC guidelines.
