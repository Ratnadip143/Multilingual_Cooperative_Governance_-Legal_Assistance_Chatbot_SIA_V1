# Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM)

## Scheme ID

27

## Official Name

Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM)

## Type

Centrally Sponsored Scheme

## Ministry / Department

Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India

## Purpose

Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM) aims to reduce rural poverty by helping poor rural households access sustainable livelihoods, financial services, skills, self-employment and employment opportunities.

The mission focuses strongly on organizing rural women into Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and strengthening community institutions.

## Key Objectives

* Reduce rural poverty
* Organize rural poor households into Self-Help Groups
* Strengthen SHG and community institutions
* Improve access to financial services
* Promote sustainable livelihoods
* Support self-employment and enterprise development
* Improve access to skills and employment opportunities
* Strengthen rural producer groups and organizations
* Improve income opportunities for rural households

## Major Features

DAY-NRLM works through community-based institutions and supports rural poor households through:

* Self-Help Groups (SHGs)
* Village-level organizations
* Cluster-level organizations
* Community resource persons
* Livelihood support
* Financial inclusion
* Skill development
* Enterprise promotion
* Market access

## Self-Help Groups

A major component of DAY-NRLM is the formation and strengthening of Self-Help Groups among rural women.

SHGs enable members to:

* Save money collectively
* Access credit
* Improve financial management
* Undertake livelihood activities
* Develop enterprises
* Access government and financial services
* Build collective bargaining capacity

## Eligible Beneficiaries

DAY-NRLM primarily focuses on poor and vulnerable rural households.

The mission places particular emphasis on:

* Rural women
* Poor rural households
* Members of eligible Self-Help Groups
* Vulnerable households
* Rural entrepreneurs
* Eligible rural youth under relevant components

Final eligibility depends on the applicable programme and State implementation guidelines.

## Role of Self-Help Groups

SHGs are an important institutional platform under DAY-NRLM.

They help members collectively:

* Save
* Borrow
* Plan livelihood activities
* Access financial services
* Develop enterprises
* Improve income opportunities
* Connect with government programmes and markets

## Application / Access

DAY-NRLM is implemented through State Rural Livelihoods Missions and local community institutions.

Rural households interested in accessing DAY-NRLM support can approach:

* Self-Help Groups
* Village-level organizations
* Gram Panchayat
* Block-level rural livelihood mission office
* State Rural Livelihoods Mission
* District Rural Development authorities
* Other designated implementing agencies

The exact process varies by State and programme component.

## Documents / Information

Depending on the programme component, applicants or SHG members may need:

* Household details
* Identity information
* Bank account details
* SHG membership information
* Mobile number
* Livelihood or enterprise details
* Project or business plan where applicable
* Other information required by the implementing authority

Exact document requirements vary according to the State and programme component.

## Implementation

DAY-NRLM is implemented through a multi-level institutional structure involving:

* Ministry of Rural Development
* State Rural Livelihoods Missions
* District-level agencies
* Block-level teams
* Community institutions
* Self-Help Groups

Community participation is an important part of programme implementation.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is DAY-NRLM?

DAY-NRLM is a rural livelihoods mission of the Government of India that aims to reduce rural poverty by supporting livelihoods, Self-Help Groups, financial inclusion, skills and rural enterprises.

### What does DAY-NRLM stand for?

DAY-NRLM stands for Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission.

### Who benefits from DAY-NRLM?

The mission primarily focuses on poor and vulnerable rural households, especially rural women organized into Self-Help Groups.

### What is the role of SHGs under DAY-NRLM?

SHGs help rural women collectively save, access credit, undertake livelihood activities, develop enterprises and access financial and government services.

### Does DAY-NRLM provide financial support?

Depending on the applicable component, DAY-NRLM can facilitate financial inclusion, access to credit and other forms of support for eligible SHGs and rural households.

### Can women join an SHG under DAY-NRLM?

Eligible rural women can participate in Self-Help Groups formed and supported through the rural livelihoods mission.

### Does DAY-NRLM support rural businesses?

Yes. Applicable components can support rural enterprises, entrepreneurship, training, technical assistance and market linkages.

### Does DAY-NRLM support agriculture and dairy activities?

Yes. Livelihood support can include agriculture, livestock, dairy, fisheries and other activities depending on State programmes and applicable guidelines.

### Does DAY-NRLM provide skill training?

Relevant programme components can provide or facilitate skill development and employment-related training.

### How can a rural household access DAY-NRLM support?

A household can approach an SHG, local rural livelihoods mission office, Gram Panchayat or other designated implementing authority.

### Is DAY-NRLM available in all States?

DAY-NRLM is a national rural livelihoods programme implemented through State-level rural livelihoods missions, subject to applicable programme arrangements.
