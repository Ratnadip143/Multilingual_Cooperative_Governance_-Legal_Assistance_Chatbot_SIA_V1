# Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH)

## Scheme ID
14

## Official Name
Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH)

## Type
Centrally Sponsored Scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

## Purpose
The Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH) aims to promote the holistic development of the horticulture sector in India.

The mission supports the production, productivity, quality and post-harvest management of horticultural crops and helps improve farmers' income through better horticulture practices and infrastructure.

## Key Objectives
- Promote holistic growth of the horticulture sector
- Increase production and productivity of horticultural crops
- Improve the quality of horticultural produce
- Promote efficient use of resources and modern technologies
- Strengthen post-harvest management
- Develop horticulture infrastructure
- Improve market access for horticultural produce
- Support farmers and horticulture-based livelihoods

## Major Horticulture Areas
MIDH covers eligible horticulture activities related to:

- Fruits
- Vegetables
- Root and tuber crops
- Mushrooms
- Spices
- Flowers
- Plantation crops
- Aromatic plants
- Other eligible horticultural crops

## Major Components
Support under MIDH may cover activities such as:

- Establishment of new horticulture gardens
- Rejuvenation of old and unproductive orchards
- Protected cultivation
- Nursery development
- Planting material production
- Post-harvest management
- Cold storage and related infrastructure
- Pack houses
- Collection and handling infrastructure
- Training and capacity building
- Other eligible horticulture development activities

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Depending on the component and applicable guidelines, beneficiaries may include:

- Individual farmers
- Small and marginal farmers
- Farmer groups
- Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
- Cooperatives
- Self Help Groups (SHGs)
- Entrepreneurs
- Other eligible horticulture-related organisations

## Benefits
- Support for horticulture production
- Improved productivity and quality
- Assistance for eligible horticulture infrastructure
- Support for nursery and planting material development
- Improved post-harvest management
- Better storage and handling facilities
- Support for protected cultivation
- Training and capacity building
- Improved market opportunities

## Post-Harvest Management
MIDH supports eligible post-harvest infrastructure to reduce losses and improve handling of horticultural produce.

Depending on the applicable component, this may include:

- Pack houses
- Cold storage
- Sorting and grading
- Transportation-related infrastructure
- Other eligible post-harvest facilities

## Application / Access
Implementation of MIDH is carried out through the concerned State Horticulture Departments and designated implementing agencies.

Eligible applicants can apply through the applicable State-level horticulture programme and implementing authority.

The application procedure, assistance and eligible activities depend on the relevant component and current guidelines.

## Documents / Information
Applicants may be required to provide:

- Applicant / beneficiary details
- Identity and address information
- Land or project details, where applicable
- Bank account details
- Project proposal or activity details
- Cost estimates
- Other documents required by the implementing agency

Exact document requirements vary according to the component and State.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is MIDH?
MIDH stands for Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture. It is a government mission for the holistic development of the horticulture sector.

### What is the main purpose of MIDH?
The main purpose is to increase horticulture production and productivity while improving quality, post-harvest management and infrastructure.

### Which crops are covered under MIDH?
Eligible horticultural crops include fruits, vegetables, root and tuber crops, mushrooms, spices, flowers, plantation crops, aromatic plants and other specified horticultural crops.

### Who can benefit from MIDH?
Eligible farmers, farmer groups, FPOs, cooperatives, SHGs, entrepreneurs and other approved beneficiaries can receive support under applicable components.

### Does MIDH support farmers?
Yes. MIDH supports eligible horticulture development activities undertaken by farmers and other approved beneficiaries.

### Does MIDH support protected cultivation?
Yes. Protected cultivation is among the horticulture development activities supported under applicable MIDH components.

### Does MIDH support cold storage?
Eligible post-harvest and cold-storage-related infrastructure can receive support under applicable components and guidelines.

### Does MIDH support nurseries?
Yes. Nursery development and production of quality planting material are among the supported horticulture activities.

### Does MIDH help reduce post-harvest losses?
Yes. Support for post-harvest management infrastructure is intended to improve handling and reduce losses of horticultural produce.

### Where can farmers apply for MIDH?
Farmers can approach the concerned State Horticulture Department or designated implementing agency for the applicable MIDH component.

### Does MIDH provide the same assistance for every activity?
No. Assistance varies according to the activity, beneficiary category, component and applicable government guidelines.

## Important Note
MIDH assistance, eligible activities, cost norms, beneficiary contribution, subsidy rates and implementation procedures may vary by component and State.

SIA should use the latest official MIDH guidelines and State-level information when answering questions about current assistance or application procedures.
