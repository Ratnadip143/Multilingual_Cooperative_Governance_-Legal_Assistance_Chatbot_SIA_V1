# National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)

## Scheme ID
29

## Official Name
National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)

## Type
Centrally Sponsored Scheme

## Ministry / Department
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India

## Purpose
The National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) provides social assistance to eligible poor and vulnerable persons, particularly elderly persons, widows and persons with disabilities.

The programme aims to provide basic income support to vulnerable households and improve social security for eligible beneficiaries.

## Key Objectives
- Provide social assistance to eligible vulnerable persons
- Support elderly persons with limited financial resources
- Provide assistance to eligible widows
- Support eligible persons with severe or multiple disabilities
- Provide assistance to bereaved households under applicable provisions
- Strengthen social security for poor and vulnerable households

## Major Components
NSAP includes the following major components:

- Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)
- Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS)
- Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (IGNDPS)
- National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS)
- Annapurna Scheme

The eligibility conditions and assistance under each component are governed by the applicable government guidelines.

## Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme
IGNOAPS provides social assistance to eligible elderly persons from poor households.

Eligibility and assistance are determined according to the applicable government criteria and current guidelines.

## Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme
IGNWPS provides social assistance to eligible widows belonging to poor households.

The applicable age, eligibility and assistance conditions are determined according to current government guidelines.

## Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme
IGNDPS provides assistance to eligible persons with severe or multiple disabilities who meet the applicable poverty and eligibility criteria.

The exact eligibility requirements are governed by the current programme guidelines.

## National Family Benefit Scheme
NFBS provides assistance to an eligible household following the death of the primary breadwinner, subject to the applicable eligibility conditions.

The assistance is intended to provide support to a household facing financial hardship because of the death of the breadwinner.

## Annapurna Scheme
The Annapurna Scheme provides food-grain support to eligible elderly persons who meet the applicable conditions and are not receiving the old-age pension under the relevant arrangements.

The availability and implementation of benefits follow current government guidelines.

## Benefits
Depending on the NSAP component, eligible beneficiaries may receive:
- Social assistance
- Pension support
- Financial assistance to eligible households
- Food-grain support under applicable provisions

The exact amount and form of assistance depend on the specific NSAP component and current government rules.

## Eligible Beneficiaries
NSAP primarily targets poor and vulnerable persons and households.

Potential beneficiary groups include:
- Eligible elderly persons
- Eligible widows
- Eligible persons with severe or multiple disabilities
- Eligible households affected by the death of a primary breadwinner
- Eligible elderly persons covered under the Annapurna component

Final eligibility depends on the applicable scheme component and government criteria.

## Central and State Assistance
NSAP is implemented through the Central Government in coordination with State Governments and Union Territories.

States and Union Territories may provide additional assistance from their own resources in addition to central assistance, subject to their applicable schemes and policies.

## Application / Access
Eligible persons should generally approach the relevant local authority for information about NSAP benefits.

Depending on the State or Union Territory, access may involve:
- Gram Panchayat
- Block Development Office
- Local government authority
- Social welfare department
- Rural development department
- Designated State or Union Territory implementing agency

The exact application and verification process varies by State and scheme component.

## Documents / Information
Applicants may be required to provide information such as:
- Identity details
- Age or date-of-birth information
- Address details
- Bank account details
- Disability-related documents where applicable
- Death certificate or related information for NFBS where applicable
- Proof of eligibility
- Other documents required by the implementing authority

Exact requirements vary according to the NSAP component and State or Union Territory procedures.

## Implementation
NSAP is implemented through State Governments and Union Territory administrations with support from the Ministry of Rural Development.

Local bodies and designated government agencies may participate in beneficiary identification, verification, payment and monitoring.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is NSAP?
NSAP stands for National Social Assistance Programme. It provides social assistance to eligible poor and vulnerable persons and households.

### What does NSAP stand for?
NSAP stands for National Social Assistance Programme.

### Who can benefit from NSAP?
Eligible elderly persons, widows, persons with severe or multiple disabilities and eligible households covered by the National Family Benefit Scheme may benefit according to the applicable criteria.

### What schemes are included under NSAP?
Major NSAP components include old-age pension, widow pension, disability pension, National Family Benefit Scheme and Annapurna.

### Does NSAP provide pension support?
Yes. Eligible beneficiaries can receive pension assistance under applicable NSAP pension components.

### Who can receive old-age pension under NSAP?
Eligible elderly persons from households meeting the applicable government criteria may receive assistance under the old-age pension component.

### Does NSAP support widows?
Yes. Eligible widows may receive assistance under the Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme according to applicable criteria.

### Does NSAP support persons with disabilities?
Yes. Eligible persons with severe or multiple disabilities may receive assistance under the applicable disability pension component.

### What is the National Family Benefit Scheme?
NFBS provides assistance to an eligible household following the death of the primary breadwinner, subject to applicable conditions.

### Where can I apply for NSAP?
Applicants should contact the Gram Panchayat, Block Development Office, State social welfare or rural development department, or another designated local authority.

### Is NSAP available across India?
NSAP is a national social assistance programme implemented through States and Union Territories according to applicable arrangements.

### Does every NSAP beneficiary receive the same amount?
No. Assistance depends on the specific NSAP component and applicable government and State or Union Territory provisions.

## Important Note
NSAP contains multiple components with different eligibility requirements and benefits.

Eligibility, pension amounts, application procedures and implementation arrangements can change according to government decisions and State or Union Territory rules.

SIA should identify the specific NSAP component being asked about and use the latest official government information.
