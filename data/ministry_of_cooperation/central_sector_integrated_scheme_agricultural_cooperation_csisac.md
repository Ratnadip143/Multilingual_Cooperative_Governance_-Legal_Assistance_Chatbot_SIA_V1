# Central Sector Integrated Scheme on Agricultural Cooperation (CSISAC)

## Scheme ID
31

## Official Name
Central Sector Integrated Scheme on Agricultural Cooperation (CSISAC)

## Type
Central Sector Scheme

## Ministry / Department
Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India

## Purpose
The Central Sector Integrated Scheme on Agricultural Cooperation (CSISAC) supports the development and strengthening of agricultural cooperatives and related cooperative activities.

The scheme aims to improve cooperative infrastructure, strengthen cooperative institutions and support agricultural development through cooperative organizations.

## Key Objectives
- Strengthen agricultural cooperative institutions
- Improve cooperative infrastructure
- Support agricultural cooperative activities
- Improve the capacity of cooperative organizations
- Promote cooperative development in the agricultural sector
- Support services and facilities for cooperative members
- Improve the effectiveness of agricultural cooperatives
- Encourage coordinated development through cooperatives

## Major Focus Areas
CSISAC may support activities related to:
- Agricultural cooperatives
- Cooperative infrastructure
- Storage and related facilities
- Processing and marketing activities
- Cooperative development
- Capacity building
- Institutional strengthening
- Other approved agricultural cooperative activities

The exact activities and assistance depend on the applicable scheme components and government guidelines.

## Beneficiaries
Potential beneficiaries may include eligible:
- Agricultural cooperative societies
- Cooperative organizations
- Cooperative federations
- Farmers associated with eligible cooperatives
- Other eligible cooperative institutions

Eligibility depends on the specific component and applicable government provisions.

## Benefits / Support
Depending on the approved activity, support may include:
- Financial assistance
- Infrastructure development support
- Institutional strengthening
- Capacity building
- Support for agricultural cooperative activities
- Assistance for approved cooperative development projects

The exact amount, type and conditions of assistance depend on the applicable component and current guidelines.

## Cooperative Infrastructure
The scheme can support the development or strengthening of infrastructure required for agricultural cooperative activities.

Infrastructure may include facilities related to:
- Storage
- Processing
- Marketing
- Collection
- Other approved cooperative agricultural activities

## Implementation
CSISAC is implemented through the Ministry of Cooperation and designated cooperative institutions or implementing agencies according to applicable government arrangements.

Implementation may involve:
- Central Government
- State Governments
- Cooperative institutions
- Cooperative federations
- Other designated agencies

## Application / Access
Access to assistance depends on the relevant CSISAC component and implementing arrangement.

Eligible cooperative organizations should approach the designated implementing authority or concerned cooperative department for information about:
- Eligibility
- Application procedure
- Approved activities
- Required documents
- Financial assistance
- Current guidelines

The procedure may vary according to the specific programme component.

## Documents / Information
Depending on the activity, applicants may be required to provide:
- Registration details of the cooperative
- Cooperative society documents
- Project proposal
- Financial information
- Details of proposed activities
- Infrastructure or project requirements
- Bank account information
- Other documents specified by the implementing authority

Exact requirements depend on the applicable component.

## Role in Agricultural Cooperation
CSISAC supports the broader objective of strengthening agricultural cooperation by improving cooperative institutions and enabling eligible organizations to undertake approved development activities.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is CSISAC?
CSISAC stands for Central Sector Integrated Scheme on Agricultural Cooperation. It supports the development and strengthening of agricultural cooperatives and related cooperative activities.

### What does CSISAC stand for?
CSISAC stands for Central Sector Integrated Scheme on Agricultural Cooperation.

### What is the main purpose of CSISAC?
Its main purpose is to strengthen agricultural cooperatives, improve cooperative infrastructure and support approved agricultural cooperative activities.

### Who can benefit from CSISAC?
Eligible agricultural cooperative societies, cooperative organizations and other approved cooperative institutions may benefit according to the applicable scheme provisions.

### What type of support can CSISAC provide?
Depending on the applicable component, support may include financial assistance, infrastructure support, capacity building and institutional strengthening.
