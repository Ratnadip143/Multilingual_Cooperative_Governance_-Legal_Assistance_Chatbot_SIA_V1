# Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)

## Scheme ID
16

## Official Name
Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)

## Type
Centrally Sponsored Scheme / Central Sector Scheme

## Ministry / Organisation
Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying

## Purpose
The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) aims to promote sustainable and responsible development of the fisheries sector in India.

The scheme supports fisheries production, productivity, infrastructure, post-harvest management, quality improvement, employment and income opportunities for people involved in fisheries and related activities.

## Key Objectives
- Increase fish production and productivity
- Promote sustainable fisheries development
- Improve fisheries infrastructure
- Strengthen post-harvest management
- Reduce post-harvest losses
- Improve quality and value addition of fish products
- Promote employment and income opportunities
- Strengthen fisheries value chains
- Support fishers, fish farmers and fisheries-related organisations
- Improve access to markets

## Major Areas of Support
Depending on applicable components and guidelines, PMMSY may support:

- Fisheries production and productivity
- Aquaculture development
- Fish seed production
- Fishing and aquaculture infrastructure
- Post-harvest management
- Cold-chain facilities
- Fish transportation
- Fish landing and handling facilities
- Fish markets
- Processing and value addition
- Fisheries extension and training
- Fishers and fish farmers welfare
- Fisheries cooperatives and other eligible organisations

- Ice plants

Eligible fisheries cooperative societies may receive support under applicable PMMSY components.

## Application / Access
Implementation of PMMSY is carried out through the Department of Fisheries and concerned State/UT fisheries departments and designated implementing agencies.

Eligible applicants should approach the concerned State/UT Fisheries Department or designated authority for the applicable component.

The application procedure, assistance and required documents depend on the beneficiary category and component.

## Documents / Information
Applicants may be required to provide:

- Applicant or beneficiary details
- Identity and address information
- Bank account details
- Fisheries or aquaculture activity details
- Land or project details, where applicable
- Project proposal
- Cost estimates
- Registration details, where applicable
- Other documents required by the implementing authority

Exact requirements may vary according to the component and State/UT.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is PMMSY?
PMMSY stands for Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana. It is a government scheme for sustainable and responsible development of the fisheries sector.

### What is the main purpose of PMMSY?
The main purpose is to increase fisheries production and productivity while strengthening infrastructure, post-harvest management, value addition and livelihoods.

### Who can benefit from PMMSY?
Eligible fishers, fish farmers, fish workers, entrepreneurs, cooperatives, SHGs, FPOs, FFPOs and other approved beneficiaries can receive support under applicable components.

### Does PMMSY support fish farmers?
Yes. Eligible fish farmers and aquaculture activities can receive support under applicable PMMSY components.

### Does PMMSY support fisheries infrastructure?
Yes. Eligible fisheries infrastructure and related facilities can receive support under applicable components.

### Does PMMSY support cold storage?
Eligible cold-chain and post-harvest infrastructure can receive support under applicable components and guidelines.

### Does PMMSY support fish processing?
Yes. Eligible processing and value-addition activities can receive support under applicable components.

### Does PMMSY support fisheries cooperatives?
Eligible fisheries cooperative societies can receive support under applicable components.

### Does PMMSY support fish transportation?
Eligible fish transportation and cold-chain-related infrastructure may receive support under applicable components.

### Where can applicants apply for PMMSY?
Applicants should approach the concerned State/UT Fisheries Department or designated implementing authority for the applicable PMMSY component.

### Does PMMSY provide the same assistance for every activity?
No. Assistance varies according to the component, beneficiary category, project and applicable government guidelines.
