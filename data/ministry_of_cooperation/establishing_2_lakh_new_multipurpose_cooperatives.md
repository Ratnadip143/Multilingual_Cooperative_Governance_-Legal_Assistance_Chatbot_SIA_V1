# Establishing 2 Lakh New Multipurpose PACS / Dairy / Fishery Cooperatives

## Scheme ID
10

## Official Name
Establishing 2 Lakh New Multipurpose PACS / Dairy / Fishery Cooperatives

## Type
Cooperative sector expansion / rural livelihood initiative

## Ministry / Organisations Involved
- Ministry of Cooperation
- Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying
- State Governments
- National Dairy Development Board (NDDB)
- National Fisheries Development Board (NFDB)
- National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)

## Purpose
The initiative aims to establish new Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), dairy cooperative societies and fishery cooperative societies in uncovered villages and Panchayats.

The objective is to strengthen the cooperative network and provide rural citizens and farmers with access to cooperative institutions and related services.

## Key Target
The initiative aims to establish approximately 2 lakh new multipurpose PACS, dairy cooperative societies and fishery cooperative societies over a five-year period.

## Main Objectives
- Expand the cooperative network in rural areas
- Cover villages and Panchayats that do not have suitable cooperative societies
- Improve access to agricultural, dairy and fisheries related services
- Create additional livelihood opportunities
- Strengthen the rural cooperative ecosystem
- Promote inclusive cooperative development

## PACS
Primary Agricultural Credit Societies can provide multiple services to farmers and rural citizens.

New PACS are intended to be established in uncovered areas and can undertake diversified activities according to the applicable model bye-laws and government guidelines.

## Dairy Cooperatives
New dairy cooperative societies can help farmers engaged in dairy activities by providing a cooperative platform for milk collection, processing, marketing and related services.

## Fishery Cooperatives
New fishery cooperative societies can support people involved in fisheries and related activities and strengthen access to cooperative services and livelihood opportunities.

## Beneficiaries
- Farmers
- Rural citizens
- Dairy farmers
- Fishermen and fishery workers
- Members of new cooperative societies
- Rural communities in uncovered villages and Panchayats

## Benefits
- Greater access to cooperative institutions
- Improved access to agricultural and livelihood services
- Opportunities for collective economic activities
- Better access to markets and related services
- Additional livelihood opportunities
- Strengthening of rural cooperative infrastructure

## Implementation
The initiative is implemented through coordination among the Central Government, State Governments and relevant cooperative and sectoral organisations.

PACS, dairy cooperative societies and fishery cooperative societies are to be established in areas where such cooperative institutions are not adequately available.

## Important Note
The exact activities, eligibility, implementation arrangements and services of individual cooperative societies depend on applicable government guidelines, state-level implementation and the relevant cooperative framework.

The target of approximately 2 lakh new cooperative societies refers to the combined initiative covering PACS, dairy cooperatives and fishery cooperatives.
