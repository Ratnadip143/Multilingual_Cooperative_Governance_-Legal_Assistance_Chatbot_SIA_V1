# Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

## Scheme ID
25

## Official Name
Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

## Type
Centrally Sponsored Scheme

## Ministry / Department
Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India

## Purpose
Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) aims to provide a pucca house with basic amenities to eligible rural households that are houseless or living in inadequate housing conditions.

## Key Objectives
- Provide pucca houses to eligible rural households
- Improve housing conditions in rural areas
- Support basic amenities along with rural housing
- Improve the quality of life of rural households
- Give priority to vulnerable and disadvantaged households

## Major Features
- Financial assistance for construction of a pucca house
- Support for basic household amenities through convergence with other government programmes
- Preference for eligible houseless and inadequately housed rural households
- Use of technology for beneficiary identification, monitoring and implementation

## Benefits
Eligible beneficiaries may receive financial assistance for construction of a pucca house.

The assistance amount and other support are governed by the applicable PMAY-G guidelines and government decisions.

Additional benefits may be provided through convergence with other government programmes for facilities such as sanitation, electricity, LPG and drinking water.

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Beneficiary selection is based on the applicable government criteria and verified rural household data.

Priority is generally given to:
- Houseless households
- Households living in houses with inadequate wall or roof conditions
- Vulnerable households
- Other households identified as eligible under the applicable PMAY-G selection process

The final eligibility and beneficiary list is determined through the official government process.

## House Requirements
The scheme supports construction of a pucca house with basic amenities.

The house design and minimum requirements follow applicable PMAY-G guidelines.

## Application / Access
PMAY-G beneficiaries are identified through the official rural housing beneficiary selection process.

Eligible households should contact:
- Gram Panchayat
- Block Development Office
- District Rural Development Agency
- Concerned State Rural Development / Panchayati Raj authorities

Beneficiary details and scheme implementation can also be checked through the official PMAY-G system.

## Documents / Information
Depending on the implementation process, beneficiaries may need information such as:
- Household details
- Beneficiary identification details
- Aadhaar-related information where applicable
- Bank account details
- Mobile number
- Address and household information
- Other information required by the implementing authority

Exact requirements may vary according to government guidelines and verification procedures.

## Implementation
PMAY-G is implemented through the rural development administration of States and Union Territories.

Gram Panchayats and other designated authorities participate in beneficiary identification, verification, monitoring and implementation.

Technology-based systems are used for monitoring construction and fund release.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is PMAY-G?
PMAY-G is a government rural housing scheme that provides financial assistance to eligible rural households for construction of pucca houses.

### Who can benefit from PMAY-G?
Eligible rural households that are houseless or living in inadequate housing conditions may benefit according to the official beneficiary selection criteria.

### Does PMAY-G provide money for house construction?
Yes. Eligible beneficiaries receive financial assistance according to the applicable government guidelines.

### How are PMAY-G beneficiaries selected?
Beneficiaries are identified through the official government process using applicable rural household data and eligibility criteria.

### Where can I ask about PMAY-G?
A person can approach the concerned Gram Panchayat, Block Development Office or other designated rural development authority.

### Can a houseless rural family get PMAY-G assistance?
A houseless rural household may be eligible, subject to the official eligibility and beneficiary selection process.

### Does PMAY-G provide basic amenities?
PMAY-G focuses on rural housing and may provide convergence with other government programmes for basic amenities.

### Is PMAY-G available in urban areas?
No. PMAY-G is specifically focused on rural housing.
