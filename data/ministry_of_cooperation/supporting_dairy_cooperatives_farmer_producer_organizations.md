# Supporting Dairy Cooperatives & Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)

## Scheme ID
24

## Official Name
Supporting Dairy Cooperatives and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)

## Type
Central Sector Scheme / Government Support Initiative

## Ministry / Organisation
Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying

## Purpose
The Government of India supports dairy cooperatives and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) to strengthen the dairy value chain and improve the economic condition of milk producers.

The support focuses on improving milk procurement, processing, marketing, infrastructure, financial stability and access to organized dairy markets.

## Key Objectives
- Strengthen dairy cooperatives
- Support dairy FPOs and milk producer organisations
- Improve milk procurement systems
- Strengthen dairy processing and infrastructure
- Improve milk quality
- Improve access to organized markets
- Promote value addition in dairy products
- Strengthen financial and institutional capacity
- Improve income opportunities for milk producers
- Support sustainable dairy value chains

## Major Areas of Support
Depending on the applicable programme and current guidelines, support may cover:

- Milk procurement infrastructure
- Milk collection systems
- Milk chilling facilities
- Dairy processing
- Milk testing
- Dairy product value addition
- Marketing infrastructure
- Cold-chain facilities
- Institutional strengthening
- Capacity building
- Training and technical support
- Financial support for eligible activities

## Dairy Cooperatives
Dairy cooperatives bring milk producers together for organized procurement, processing and marketing.

Government programmes may support eligible dairy cooperatives to improve:

- Milk collection
- Procurement
- Chilling
- Processing
- Testing
- Marketing
- Dairy product value addition
- Institutional capacity

## Farmer Producer Organizations
Farmer Producer Organizations can help farmers collectively undertake production, aggregation, processing and marketing activities.

Eligible dairy FPOs may receive support under applicable government programmes for approved activities.

## Benefits
Depending on the applicable programme or component, support may contribute to:

- Better milk collection
- Improved milk chilling
- Improved processing facilities
- Better milk quality
- Improved market access
- Value addition
- Stronger dairy institutions
- Better technical capacity
- Improved bargaining power
- Better income opportunities for milk producers

The exact assistance depends on the applicable programme and current government guidelines.

## Eligible Beneficiaries
Depending on the applicable programme, eligible beneficiaries or institutions may include:

- Dairy cooperative societies
- Milk unions
- State dairy federations
- Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
- Milk producer companies
- Producer groups
- Other eligible dairy institutions

Eligibility depends on the specific programme and applicable guidelines.

## Support for Milk Producers
Milk producers may benefit through stronger dairy cooperatives and FPOs.

Improved collective procurement, processing and marketing can help producers access organized markets and better dairy value chains.

## Infrastructure Support
Eligible dairy institutions may receive support under applicable programmes for infrastructure such as:

- Milk collection centres
- Bulk milk coolers
- Milk chilling facilities
- Dairy processing plants
- Milk testing laboratories
- Cold-chain infrastructure
- Milk transportation
- Packaging and value-addition facilities

## Institutional Strengthening
Government support may also focus on strengthening the organizational and technical capacity of dairy cooperatives and FPOs.

This may include:

- Training
- Capacity building
- Technical assistance
- Management improvement
- Digital systems
- Better record keeping
- Market development

## Application / Access
Eligible dairy cooperatives, FPOs and other institutions should follow the application process applicable to the relevant government programme.

Applicants may need to approach the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, concerned State department, dairy federation, implementing agency or other designated authority.

The procedure depends on the specific programme and type of support requested.

## Documents / Information
Institutional applicants may be required to provide:

- Organisation details
- Registration documents
- Details of members or milk producers
- Project proposal
- Project cost estimates
- Financial information
- Bank details
- Infrastructure details
- Business or activity plan
- Other documents required by the implementing authority

Exact requirements vary according to the programme and activity.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What support is available for dairy cooperatives?
Eligible dairy cooperatives may receive support under applicable government programmes for infrastructure, milk procurement, processing, marketing, capacity building and institutional strengthening.

### What is the role of dairy cooperatives?
Dairy cooperatives help milk producers collectively procure, process and market milk and dairy products.

### Can FPOs receive support in the dairy sector?
Eligible dairy FPOs may receive support under applicable government programmes for approved dairy activities.

### What are the benefits of joining a dairy cooperative?
Members may gain access to organized milk procurement, collection, chilling, processing, marketing and other dairy services.

### Can dairy cooperatives receive infrastructure support?
Yes. Eligible infrastructure such as milk collection, chilling, testing and processing facilities may be supported under applicable programmes.

### Does government support milk processing?
Eligible dairy processing activities can receive support under applicable government schemes and programmes.

### Does this support improve market access?
Yes. Strengthening procurement, processing and marketing systems can improve access to organized dairy markets.

### Can milk producers benefit through FPOs?
Yes. Eligible milk producers can benefit through collective procurement, processing, marketing and other services provided by their FPO.

### Who can apply for institutional support?
Eligible dairy cooperatives, FPOs, milk unions, federations and other approved institutions can apply under the relevant programme.

### Where can dairy cooperatives or FPOs seek support?
They should approach the concerned Department, State implementing agency, dairy federation or designated authority for the relevant programme.

### Is the same support available to every cooperative or FPO?
No. Eligibility and assistance depend on the specific programme, activity, institution and current government guidelines.

## Important Note
There are multiple government programmes supporting dairy cooperatives and FPOs. Eligibility, funding, assistance and application procedures vary between programmes.

SIA should identify the specific programme being asked about and use the latest official government information rather than presenting all dairy cooperative support as one uniform scheme.
