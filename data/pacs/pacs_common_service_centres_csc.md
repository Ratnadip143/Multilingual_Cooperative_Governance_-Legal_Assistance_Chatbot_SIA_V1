# PACS to Function as Common Service Centres (CSC)

## Scheme ID
06

## Official Name
PACS to Function as Common Service Centres (CSC)

## Type
PACS service diversification / e-Governance service delivery initiative

## Ministries / Organisations Involved
- Ministry of Cooperation
- Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
- National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)
- CSC e-Governance Services India Limited (CSC-SPV)

## Purpose
Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) are enabled to function as Common Service Centres (CSCs) so that rural citizens can access a wide range of government-to-citizen and other e-governance services at the village level.

The initiative also helps PACS diversify their economic activities and develop additional and sustainable sources of income.

## Key Feature
PACS functioning as CSCs can provide more than 300 e-services through the CSC Digital Seva Portal.

## Major Services
Services available through PACS functioning as CSCs include:

- Banking services
- Insurance services
- Aadhaar enrolment and updation
- Health services
- Agricultural services
- Legal services
- Electricity and water bill payment services
- PAN card related services
- Jeevan Pramaan
- Passport-related services
- Income Tax Return (ITR) filing
- E-Stamp services
- IRCTC / bus / air ticket services
- Other citizen-centric e-governance services

## Government Welfare and Agricultural Services
PACS functioning as CSCs can also provide access to services related to government schemes, including:

- Ayushman Bharat Yojana
- PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana
- Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
- e-Shram registration
- PM-KISAN

## Beneficiaries
- Rural citizens
- Farmers
- PACS members
- Other citizens requiring government and digital services in rural areas

## Benefits to Rural Citizens
- Easier access to government services at the village level
- Reduced need to travel to distant service centres
- Improved access to banking and insurance services
- Easier access to agricultural and welfare services
- Access to digital and citizen-centric services through PACS

## Benefits to PACS
- Diversification of PACS economic activities
- Additional sources of income
- More sustainable revenue opportunities
- Greater role in rural service delivery
- Strengthening of PACS as village-level service centres

## Implementation
An MoU was signed between the Ministry of Cooperation, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, NABARD and CSC e-Governance Services India Limited to enable PACS to provide CSC services.

A nodal officer is to be appointed by the State Government for effective implementation of the initiative.

PACS/LAMPS are encouraged to provide CSC services to rural citizens.

## Eligibility / Access
The initiative is implemented through eligible PACS functioning as Common Service Centres. Citizens can access the available CSC services through participating PACS.

Specific availability of an individual service may depend on the PACS and the CSC services enabled there.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### Can PACS function as Common Service Centres?
Yes. PACS have been enabled to function as Common Service Centres (CSCs).

### What is the purpose of PACS functioning as CSCs?
The purpose is to provide digital, government and citizen-centric services to rural citizens at the village level while also helping PACS diversify their activities and income sources.

### How many services can PACS provide through CSCs?
PACS functioning as CSCs can provide more than 300 e-services through the CSC Digital Seva Portal.

### What services are available through PACS as CSCs?
Services include banking, insurance, Aadhaar services, health services, agricultural services, legal services and various government-to-citizen services.

### Can farmers access agricultural services through PACS as CSCs?
Yes. Agricultural services and services related to several government schemes can be provided through PACS functioning as CSCs.

### Can PMFBY services be accessed through PACS as CSCs?
PMFBY is among the government scheme-related services that PACS functioning as CSCs can provide.

### Can Aadhaar services be provided through PACS as CSCs?
Yes. Aadhaar enrolment and updation are among the listed CSC services.

### Does this initiative benefit PACS?
Yes. It helps PACS diversify their activities and provides opportunities for additional and sustainable sources of income.

### Does every PACS provide every CSC service?
Not necessarily. The availability of a particular service depends on the services enabled at the participating PACS/CSC.

## Important Note
The availability of individual CSC services and implementation status can change over time. SIA should use the latest official Ministry of Cooperation / CSC information when answering questions about current availability or status.
