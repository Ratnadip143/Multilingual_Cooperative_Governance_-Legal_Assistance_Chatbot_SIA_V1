# PACS Made Eligible for LPG Distributorship

## Scheme ID
07

## Official Name
PACS Made Eligible for LPG Distributorship

## Type
PACS diversification / rural service initiative

## Ministries / Organisations Involved
- Ministry of Cooperation
- Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas
- Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs)

## Purpose
Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) have been made eligible to apply for LPG distributorships to diversify their economic activities and create additional sources of income.

## Key Feature
PACS can participate in the LPG distributorship process subject to the applicable eligibility conditions and guidelines of the concerned Oil Marketing Companies.

## Objective
- Diversify the business activities of PACS
- Create additional income opportunities for PACS
- Improve access to LPG services in rural areas
- Strengthen PACS as multipurpose rural institutions

## Beneficiaries
- Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)
- Rural citizens who may benefit from improved LPG distribution access

## Benefits to PACS
- Opportunity to enter the LPG distribution business
- Additional source of income
- Diversification of PACS activities
- Greater role in rural economic development

## Access / Application
Eligible PACS can participate in the LPG distributorship process according to the applicable guidelines and selection process of the Oil Marketing Companies.

The exact eligibility conditions, application procedure and selection requirements may vary according to the applicable LPG distributorship guidelines.

## Important Note
Eligibility does not mean that every PACS automatically receives an LPG distributorship. PACS must satisfy the applicable conditions and go through the prescribed selection process.

Current eligibility conditions and application requirements should be verified from the latest official information issued by the Ministry of Cooperation, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, or the concerned Oil Marketing Company.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### Can PACS get an LPG distributorship?
Yes. PACS have been made eligible to apply for LPG distributorships, subject to applicable conditions and the prescribed selection process.

The initiative helps PACS diversify their activities and create additional sources of income.

No. PACS must meet the applicable eligibility conditions and go through the prescribed selection process.

### Can PACS apply for LPG distributorship?
Eligible PACS can participate in the LPG distributorship process according to the applicable guidelines.

### What is the benefit of LPG distributorship for PACS?
It provides an opportunity to diversify PACS activities and generate an additional source of income.

### Who implements LPG distributorship?
LPG distributorship is implemented through the concerned Oil Marketing Companies under the applicable LPG distribution framework.
