# PACS to Get Priority in Petrol/Diesel Dealership

## Scheme ID
08

## Official Name
PACS to Get Priority in Petrol/Diesel Dealership

## Type
PACS diversification / rural business initiative

## Ministries / Organisations Involved
- Ministry of Cooperation
- Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas
- Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs)

## Purpose
Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) have been given priority in the process for allotment of petrol and diesel retail outlets to help diversify their economic activities and create additional sources of income.

## Key Feature
PACS are given priority in the selection process for new petrol and diesel retail outlets, subject to the applicable guidelines and eligibility conditions.

## Objective
- Diversify the business activities of PACS
- Create additional income opportunities for PACS
- Strengthen PACS as multipurpose rural institutions
- Improve access to fuel services in rural areas

## Beneficiaries
- Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)
- Rural communities that may benefit from additional fuel retail outlets

## Benefits to PACS
- Priority in the process for petrol and diesel retail outlets
- Opportunity to diversify PACS activities
- Potential additional source of income
- Greater role in rural economic development

## Eligibility / Access
Eligible PACS can participate in the petrol and diesel retail outlet selection process according to the applicable guidelines and conditions.

Priority does not mean that every PACS automatically receives a dealership. PACS must satisfy the prescribed eligibility requirements and selection conditions.

## Implementation
The initiative is implemented in coordination with the concerned Oil Marketing Companies under the applicable petrol and diesel retail outlet dealership framework.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### Do PACS get priority for petrol and diesel dealerships?
Yes. PACS have been given priority in the process for allotment of petrol and diesel retail outlets, subject to applicable conditions.

### Why were PACS given priority for petrol and diesel dealerships?
The initiative helps PACS diversify their activities and create additional sources of income.

### Does every PACS automatically get a petrol or diesel dealership?
No. PACS must satisfy the applicable eligibility requirements and go through the prescribed selection process.

### Can PACS apply for petrol and diesel retail outlets?
Eligible PACS can participate in the selection process according to the applicable guidelines.

### What is the benefit of petrol and diesel dealership for PACS?
It provides PACS with an opportunity to diversify their activities and generate additional income.

### Who implements the petrol and diesel dealership process?
The process is carried out through the concerned Oil Marketing Companies under the applicable dealership guidelines.

## Important Note
Priority does not guarantee allotment of a petrol or diesel retail outlet.

Eligibility conditions, application procedures and selection rules may change. Current requirements should be verified from the latest official information issued by the Ministry of Cooperation, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, or the concerned Oil Marketing Company.
