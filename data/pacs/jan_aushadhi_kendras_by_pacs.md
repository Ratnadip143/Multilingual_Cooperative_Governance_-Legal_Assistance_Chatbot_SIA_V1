# Jan Aushadhi Kendras by PACS

## Purpose

The initiative enables Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) to operate Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Kendras (PMBJKs).

The objective is to improve access to quality generic medicines at affordable prices, particularly in rural areas, while also creating an additional source of income and employment opportunities for PACS.

## Role of PACS

PACS can operate Jan Aushadhi Kendras and provide affordable generic medicines to people in their communities.

This initiative also helps diversify the economic activities of PACS.

## Benefits

The initiative can:

1. Improve access to affordable medicines in rural areas.
2. Provide an additional source of income for PACS.
3. Create employment opportunities.
4. Expand the reach of Jan Aushadhi medicines through the cooperative network.
5. Support affordable healthcare access for rural communities.

## Initial Government Decision

In June 2023, the Government decided to allow 2,000 PACS across the country to open Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Jan Aushadhi Kendras.

The 2,000 PACS were planned to be identified for opening Jan Aushadhi Kendras across the country.

## Current Status

As of 30 June 2026, 846 Jan Aushadhi Kendras had been opened under PACS across the country.

More than 4,250 PACS had received initial approval to open Jan Aushadhi Kendras.

There is no State/UT/District-wise target for opening new Jan Aushadhi Kendras through PACS.

The Government has set a target of opening 25,000 Jan Aushadhi Kendras by March 2027.

## Benefits Available to PACS Operating Jan Aushadhi Kendras

PACS operating Jan Aushadhi Kendras are eligible for the same benefits as other Jan Aushadhi Kendras under the Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana, subject to the applicable guidelines.

These include:

- 30% trade margin on the Maximum Retail Price (MRP).
- Incentives linked to purchases of medicines.
- Incentives related to fulfilment of stocking requirements.

A special one-time grant of Rs. 2 lakh is available for eligible Jan Aushadhi Kendras in specified priority areas and for specified categories of entrepreneurs, subject to applicable conditions.

## Stocking Requirement / Incentive

Since September 2024, stocking of 200 commonly used medicines has been incentivised.

The 200 medicines consist of:

- 100 top-selling medicines in the scheme product basket.
- 100 fast-selling medicines in the market.

Jan Aushadhi Kendra owners can receive a monthly incentive based on the stocks maintained for these medicines, subject to applicable guidelines.

## Supply Chain

An information-technology-enabled supply chain system is in place for Jan Aushadhi Kendras.

The system connects warehouses and distributors to support availability of medicines and other products.

## Application / Access Route

PACS interested in operating Jan Aushadhi Kendras should follow the applicable PMBJP and Ministry of Cooperation procedures.

Current eligibility, approval, registration, drug licensing and operational requirements should be checked against the latest official PMBJP and Ministry of Cooperation guidelines.

## Eligibility / Conditions

PACS can be allowed to open and operate Jan Aushadhi Kendras subject to the applicable Government procedures and conditions.

Specific eligibility and approval requirements may change and should be verified from the latest official sources.

## Documents / Data Requirements

Specific document requirements should be checked against the current PMBJP application, approval and drug licensing requirements.

## Process Summary

1. Eligible PACS is identified / applies under the applicable process.
2. Initial approval is obtained through the relevant Government mechanism.
3. Required licensing and operational requirements are completed.
4. The PACS operates the Jan Aushadhi Kendra.
5. The Kendra supplies medicines and products under the PMBJP framework.
6. The PACS receives applicable margins and incentives subject to the scheme guidelines.
