# World's Largest Grain Storage Plan in Cooperative Sector

## Scheme ID
09

## Official Name
World's Largest Grain Storage Plan in Cooperative Sector

## Type
Cooperative sector / agricultural storage infrastructure initiative

## Ministry / Organisation
Ministry of Cooperation

## Purpose
The plan aims to create decentralized grain storage capacity at the Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS) level by establishing godowns and other agricultural infrastructure.

The initiative is intended to address the shortage of agricultural storage capacity and enable farmers to store their produce locally.

## Key Objective
- Create decentralized grain storage capacity
- Reduce post-harvest losses
- Help farmers avoid distress sales
- Improve local storage infrastructure
- Strengthen PACS as multipurpose cooperative institutions

## PACS Role
PACS are used as important points for developing decentralized grain storage infrastructure at the local level.

PACS can also undertake related agricultural infrastructure and post-harvest activities.

## Major Components
The plan provides for convergence with various existing schemes and programmes of different ministries to create agricultural infrastructure through PACS.

Infrastructure may include:
- Grain storage godowns
- Custom Hiring Centres
- Primary Processing Units
- Other agricultural infrastructure as permitted under the programme

## Benefits to Farmers
- Local storage facility closer to farms
- Opportunity to store agricultural produce
- Reduced dependence on distant storage facilities
- Reduced possibility of distress sale immediately after harvest
- Better post-harvest management

## Benefits to PACS
- Diversification of PACS activities
- Additional income-generating opportunities
- Strengthening of PACS infrastructure
- Greater role in agricultural supply-chain activities

## Implementation
The plan is implemented through convergence of existing schemes of different ministries and departments.

PACS are developed as decentralized storage and agricultural infrastructure points under the plan.

## Important Note
The plan is implemented through convergence with existing government schemes. Specific financial assistance, infrastructure components, eligibility and implementation conditions depend on the applicable scheme and current government guidelines.

## Frequently Asked Questions / Intents

### What is the World's Largest Grain Storage Plan?
It is a cooperative-sector initiative to create decentralized grain storage capacity at the PACS level.

### Why was the grain storage plan introduced?
The plan aims to address the shortage of agricultural storage capacity, reduce post-harvest losses and help farmers avoid distress sales.

### What role do PACS play in the grain storage plan?
PACS serve as important local-level institutions for developing decentralized grain storage and related agricultural infrastructure.

### Can grain storage facilities be established through PACS?
Yes. The plan focuses on creating decentralized grain storage infrastructure at the PACS level.

### How does the plan benefit farmers?
It provides local storage opportunities and can help farmers store produce instead of being forced to sell immediately after harvest.

### Does the plan only involve grain storage?
No. The programme also supports convergence for related agricultural infrastructure such as Custom Hiring Centres and Primary Processing Units.

### Does the plan provide financial assistance?
The plan uses convergence with existing government schemes. The exact assistance depends on the applicable scheme and current guidelines.

### Is the plan implemented across India?
The initiative is intended for implementation through PACS across the country, subject to applicable implementation arrangements and guidelines.
