## Purpose

The initiative aims to strengthen the cooperative sector by promoting the formation of Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) through Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS).

FPOs are member-based organizations that work to improve the economic and social status of farmers by facilitating better access to agricultural inputs, technology, markets and financial services.

The initiative is intended to empower farmers, improve collective bargaining power and support agricultural growth and sustainability.

## Background

The Government of India launched the Central Sector Scheme
"Formation and Promotion of 10,000 Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)"
in 2020.

NCDC is one of the Implementing Agencies under this scheme.

For formation and promotion of FPOs in the cooperative sector through strengthening of PACS, an additional target of FPOs was allocated to NCDC.

The Ministry of Cooperation's initiative states that 1,100 additional FPOs were allocated to NCDC for formation in the cooperative sector.

## Role of PACS

Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) can be encouraged by State Governments to set up FPOs.

Formation of FPOs through PACS can help farmers work collectively and obtain better access to:

- Agricultural inputs
- Technology
- Markets
- Financial services
- Market linkages
- Collective bargaining opportunities

## Benefits / Support

The initiative can help farmers by:

1. Improving collective bargaining power.
2. Providing better access to agricultural inputs.
3. Improving access to technology.
4. Providing better market linkages.
5. Improving access to financial services.
6. Supporting collective marketing of agricultural produce.
7. Helping farmers obtain better and more remunerative market opportunities.

## FPO Support under the Central Sector Scheme

Under the Central Sector Scheme for Formation and Promotion of 10,000 FPOs:

- Financial assistance of up to Rs. 18 lakh per FPO is provided for a period of 3 years.
- Matching equity grant support is available up to Rs. 2,000 per farmer member, subject to a maximum of Rs. 15 lakh per FPO.
- Credit guarantee facility is available up to Rs. 2 crore of project loan per FPO from eligible lending institutions.
- Cluster Based Business Organisations (CBBOs) provide professional handholding support to FPOs.

## Implementation

Formation and promotion of FPOs is carried out through Implementing Agencies (IAs).

Implementing Agencies engage Cluster Based Business Organisations (CBBOs) for formation and professional handholding support to FPOs.

NCDC is an Implementing Agency under the Central Sector Scheme.

For the cooperative-sector initiative, NCDC was allocated additional FPO targets for formation and promotion through strengthening of PACS.

## Access / Application Route

PACS may be encouraged by the State Government to set up FPOs.

The formation and promotion process is carried out through the designated Implementing Agency and the associated FPO/CBBO mechanism under the Central Sector Scheme.

For current application procedures, eligibility conditions and operational requirements, users should refer to the latest official NCDC FPO guidelines and the Ministry of Cooperation.

## Process Summary

1. Identify suitable PACS / farmer members.
2. State Government may encourage PACS to set up an FPO.
3. FPO formation is taken forward under the Central Sector Scheme.

## FAQs / Query Intents

### What is an FPO?

A Farmer Producer Organization (FPO) is a member-based organization formed to improve the economic and social status of farmers through collective activities and better access to inputs, technology, markets and financial services.

### Can a PACS form an FPO?

PACS can be encouraged by the State Government to set up FPOs under the cooperative-sector initiative.

### Why are FPOs being promoted through PACS?

FPOs through PACS can help farmers improve collective bargaining power and access inputs, technology, markets and financial services.

### Who is the implementing agency for the cooperative-sector FPO initiative?

NCDC is an Implementing Agency under the Central Sector Scheme for Formation and Promotion of 10,000 FPOs and has been allocated additional FPO targets for the cooperative sector.

### What support is available to FPOs?

Support under the Central Sector Scheme includes financial assistance, equity grant support, credit guarantee support and professional handholding through CBBOs, subject to the applicable scheme guidelines.

### How can a PACS start an FPO?

A PACS may be encouraged by the State Government to set up an FPO. The current formation and implementation process should be checked with the designated Implementing Agency and latest NCDC guidelines.

### What are the benefits of an FPO?

FPOs can provide better access to agricultural inputs, technology, markets and financial services and can improve farmers' collective bargaining power.

### Can an FPO help with market access?

Yes. FPOs are intended to provide better market linkages and support collective marketing of farmers' produce.
